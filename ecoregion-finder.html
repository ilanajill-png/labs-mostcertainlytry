Find an Address Ecoregion

Enter a U.S. address, choose the state, and get the matched latitude, longitude, and EPA terrestrial ecoregion levels.

We do not save the address, coordinates, or result.

Result

Submit an address to run the lookup.

Privacy

This page does not use a database, does not write to browser storage, and does not intentionally log submitted addresses.

What leaves the page
The address is sent to Esri's public geocoder to convert it into a coordinate.
What the EPA receives
Only the latitude and longitude are sent to the EPA ecoregion map service to identify the matching polygon.
What Labs saves
Nothing from your submission. Results are shown in your browser and disappear when you leave or refresh the page.
Addresses are sensitive. Use a nearby intersection or landmark if exact-house precision is not needed.

Sources

The lookup uses public map services and returns EPA ecoregion attributes when the coordinate intersects a mapped polygon.
