## Annual transmitter columns

The directory now includes separate columns for **2024**, **2025**, and **2026**. Each populated cell gives the transmitter count, release location, and deployment status when Project Monarch publishes one.

- **2024:** Project Monarch confirms that Cape May Point Arts & Science Center continued supporting deployments, but publishes no annual total. The only individually documented release is Lionel at Cape May Point in November 2024. The CTT and center rows describe the same joint release and must not be added together.
- **2025:** The contemporaneous press release reports **more than 400 transmitters deployed from Canada to Cuba**. The later coverage feed includes a 525-tag consortium record, but does not allocate that figure among organizations. Partner rows therefore show exact organization-level numbers only where another official source provides them.
- **2026:** Counts are targets from the live Project Monarch deployment feed, not automatically completed releases. Cells preserve the feed's status—fully deployed, partially deployed, tags in hand, funded/tags expected, or status not supplied.
- **Joint records:** A number repeated in two or more partner rows may describe one combined effort. Those cells say “joint” or “share not separated” and should not be summed across rows.
- **No organization-specific public deployment record found** means the project does not publish a count tied to that roster member. It does not prove that the organization released zero butterflies.

## How to read the participation synopsis

- **Documented deployment:** season, location, purpose, and planned tag count come from the live Project Monarch coverage feed. These are plans or targets unless the synopsis explicitly says the deployment was completed.
- **Documented infrastructure/coordination:** the role is stated on the Project Monarch website, history, or official coordinating page.
- **Roster-only:** Project Monarch publicly identifies the participant, but does not publish an organization-specific field activity. The directory says so instead of inferring a role from the organization's general mission.
- Social profiles are included only when they were linked from an official organization site or could be tied unambiguously to the organization. Parent-institution accounts are labeled in the synopsis. **Not found** means no official account was verified; it does not prove that no account exists.

## Scope distinction

The fall 2025 collaboration page described **more than 20 partners across four countries deploying more than 400 transmitters**. The current homepage has expanded to **82 listed partners**. This directory covers the current 82-member roster and does not imply that every current member participated in the original 2025 deployment.
