10. What Not To Automate

Some care should stay human.

This may be the most important section. A living system is not a fully automated system. Some things should stay tactile, chosen, slow, private, or human.

  • Do not automate anything that makes guests feel watched.
  • Do not automate personal communications without review.
  • Do not automate purchases, account signups, or public posts.
  • Do not replace human hospitality with dashboards.
  • Do not make the house louder just because it became smarter.
