Recipe 1 · Fastest build

Breakfast Burrito Brunch Board

This is the honest use of the coupon: heat the free burritos, slice them into shareable halves, and build a brunch plate that feels intentional instead of "I microwaved a thing."

Time
20 minutes
Serves
4
Meal Deal core
Breakfast burritos + coffee
Best for
Wednesday pickup breakfast or Sunday brunch
Source inspiration
The BakerMama: Build-Your-Own Breakfast Taco Board

Ingredients

  • 4 Meal Simple Breakfast Burritos
  • Brewed CAFE Ole coffee
  • Salsa and hot sauce
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • Lime wedges
  • Fresh berries or grapes
  • Cilantro and scallions, optional

Method

  1. Heat burritos according to package directions, then crisp the outside briefly in a dry skillet.
  2. Slice each burrito on a diagonal and arrange on a warm platter.
  3. Add salsa, hot sauce, avocado, lime, and fruit in small bowls or loose clusters.
  4. Serve with brewed coffee first; open sparkling only if this is a real brunch moment.

Andre/H-E-B Wine Pairing

Zardetto Prosecco or any dry H-E-B Prosecco. Andre's holiday-shopping notes treat cheap Prosecco as the correct mimosa bottle because orange juice is doing the work. For burritos, keep it dry and cold; the bubbles handle tortilla, eggs, cheese, salsa, and avocado without making brunch feel fancy in the wrong way.

Recipe 2 · Sweet breakfast bread build

Breakfast Bread French Toast with Coffee Bacon

Breakfast bread wants French toast. The coffee you bought for the deal becomes a tiny bitter note in maple bacon, which keeps the whole plate from turning into dessert cosplay.

Time
35 minutes
Serves
4
Meal Deal core
Breakfast bread + coffee
Best for
Breakfast-for-dinner
Source inspiration
The Pioneer Woman: Baked French Toast + Spicy Southern Kitchen: Bacon Stuffed French Toast

Ingredients

  • 8 thick slices H-E-B Breakfast Bread
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup milk or half-and-half
  • Vanilla, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt
  • Butter, for the skillet
  • 8 strips bacon
  • 2 Tbsp brewed CAFE Ole coffee
  • 3 Tbsp maple syrup
  • Berries or sliced peaches

Method

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt. Soak bread slices until custardy but not collapsing.
  2. Cook French toast in butter until browned and set in the center.
  3. Cook bacon until almost crisp. Brush with coffee and maple syrup; return to the pan for 1 minute per side.
  4. Serve French toast with coffee bacon, fruit, and a restrained amount of syrup.

Andre/H-E-B Wine Pairing

Dry Prosecco. Same Andre mimosa logic, but with no need to overthink it: light, fruity, injected-bubble Prosecco is exactly the lane for sweet brunch food. If serving this at night, pour small glasses and let coffee remain the actual headline.

Recipe 3 · Make-ahead savory build

Breakfast Bread Green Chile Strata

This is the best move if the free breakfast bread is not your preferred toast bread. Cube it, soak it with eggs and milk, add green chiles and cheddar, then bake it into a brunch casserole.

Time
20 minutes active + bake
Serves
6
Meal Deal core
Breakfast bread
Best for
Make-ahead breakfast or light dinner
Source inspiration
Simply Recipes: Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients

  • 10 cups cubed H-E-B Breakfast Bread
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 small cans diced green chiles
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage, browned, optional
  • Scallions, sliced
  • Cumin, black pepper, and salt

Method

  1. Butter a baking dish and fill it with cubed breakfast bread.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, green chiles, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fold in cheddar, scallions, and browned sausage if using. Pour over the bread.
  4. Rest at least 30 minutes, or refrigerate overnight. Bake at 350°F until puffed and set.
  5. Serve with salsa, avocado, or a green salad if this is dinner.

Andre/H-E-B Wine Pairing

Schmitt Sohne Dry Riesling. The Andre pattern is straightforward: eggs, cheese, green chile, and sausage need acid more than oak. Riesling gives limey lift, enough fruit for chile, and a clean finish against the custardy bread.

Recipe 4 · Breakfast-for-dinner build

Coffee-Glazed Sausage and Egg Hash

This is the most dinner-like build of the week. Coffee becomes a small glaze for sausage and potatoes, not a gimmick, and eggs make it a full skillet meal.

Time
45 minutes
Serves
4
Meal Deal core
CAFE Ole coffee
Best for
Weeknight breakfast dinner
Source inspiration
RecipeTin Eats: Baked Sausage Breakfast Hash

Ingredients

  • 12 oz breakfast sausage links, sliced
  • 1 1/2 lb potatoes, diced small
  • 1 yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/4 cup brewed CAFE Ole coffee
  • 1 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • Smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and olive oil

Method

  1. Brown sausage in a large skillet, then remove it to a plate.
  2. Cook potatoes in oil with salt and smoked paprika until browned and tender. Add onion and bell pepper.
  3. Stir coffee, maple syrup, and Dijon together. Add sausage back to the skillet and glaze for 1 to 2 minutes.
  4. Fry eggs separately or make wells in the hash and cook them in the skillet.
  5. Serve with hot sauce and a small piece of toasted breakfast bread if you have it.

Andre/H-E-B Wine Pairing

Louis Jadot Beaujolais-Villages. Andre's Beaujolais/Gamay lane is crunchy, lively, and Burgundy-adjacent without behaving like a serious steakhouse red. Served lightly chilled, it works with sausage, potatoes, eggs, coffee bitterness, and paprika.
