Today's Route

Medici Chapel, Rest, Bardini

  1. 08:45-10:15 | Cappelle Medicee. Start at Piazza di Madonna degli Aldobrandini 6. See the Cappella dei Principi and Michelangelo's Sagrestia Nuova before the heat gets rude.
  2. 10:15-11:15 | San Lorenzo / shaded coffee. Keep this as buffer. If ticketing or lines are annoying, do not punish yourself by cramming in another paid site.
  3. 11:30-16:45 | Heat retreat. Lunch, water, rest, and indoor time. The Botanical Garden and Boboli are open, but today they are optional only if your body votes yes.
  4. 17:15-17:45 | Cross toward Oltrarno. Taxi if the heat is still sharp; walk only if it feels humane. Bardini has steep, uneven, gravelly paths.
  5. 17:45-20:15 | Giardino Bardini. Enter from Costa San Giorgio 2 or Via dei Bardi 1 rosso. Aim for terrace views, shade, sculptures, the wisteria pergola structure, roses, hydrangeas, and the slow city view.
  6. 20:15 onward | Dinner or stars. Either stay simple near Oltrarno/San Niccolo, or go to the 21:00 San Miniato meadow event if you reserve and still have energy.

Verified Options For Today

Best fit with your two requested stops

  • Giardino Bardini / Villa Bardini: Thursday opening runs late to 21:00 for the exhibition, garden, and cocktail bar; last admission one hour before closing. The same site lists the garden's steep paths, no-animal rule, two entrances, and cafe/bar.
  • Cinema in Villa, Bardini: Nightly outdoor cinema runs at 21:15 through August 30 on the Terrazza Belvedere. Thursday is the family-free strand by reservation, but check the live program before committing tonight.
  • Cappelle Medicee: Not a garden, but it is the indoor Medici anchor for the day: open today 08:15-18:50, last entrance 40 minutes before close.
  • Serata sotto le stelle with Marginalia: 21:00 on the meadow below San Miniato al Monte; science, art, poetry, and stargazing. Bring a blanket or folding chair, and reserve before going.

Open green places if you want more nature

  • Boboli Gardens: open from 08:15; August closing 19:10, last admission one hour before closing. Better as an early add-on than a noon idea.
  • Botanical Garden / Giardino dei Semplici: June-August hours are Tuesday-Sunday, 10:00-19:00, with the ticket office closing one hour before. Plant-rich, central, and easier than Boboli, but still an outdoor choice in heavy heat.
  • Giardino delle Rose: open daily 09:00-20:00 in May-September. Good as a quick sunset view stop near Piazzale Michelangelo/San Miniato, not as a hard requirement.
  • Parco d'Arte Pazzagli: public listings describe a garden/sculpture park with more than 200 works and 300 cypresses; the operator notes August openings mainly Saturday/Sunday evenings, with other times by reservation. Treat any August 13 nature-lab listing as call-before-you-go.
  • Cinema nel Chiostro, Sant'Orsola: an outdoor cloister cinema runs through September 6 at 21:30. Nature-adjacent rather than a garden, but useful if you want an evening outside without another hill.
  • Spazio Giovani Cure / Fico Bistrot garden: Portalegiovani lists 2MD original music at 20:00 in the garden tonight. Neighborhood choice, not a garden destination.

Identification Guide

What You Are Likely To Notice At Bardini

Bardini's own garden description names holm oaks, phyllireas, olive trees, hydrangeas, roses, camellias, azaleas, and the wisteria pergola. In August, expect structure, leaves, shade, insects, and dry-season Mediterranean toughness more than peak spring bloom.

Plant

Wisteria pergola

Look for the long woody vine structure overhead; the famous purple bloom is mostly April-May, so today it is more architecture than flower show.

Bardini names Japanese wisteria varieties plus Wisteria sinensis 'Prolific' on the pergola.

Tree

Mediterranean cypress

Tall, dark, pencil-like silhouettes; classic Tuscan skyline punctuation.

Watch for them on views, walls, and neighboring gardens rather than only inside Bardini.

Tree

Holm oak

Evergreen oak with dense, leathery, dark leaves; it reads as deep shade in a hot Mediterranean garden.

Bardini specifically notes copses and centuries-old trees including holm oaks.

Tree

Olive

Silvery, narrow leaves; gnarled trunks; a softer gray-green than cypress or oak.

Good for training your eye to read the dry Mediterranean palette.

Plant

Bay laurel

Glossy, aromatic, lance-shaped leaves; often clipped or tucked into Mediterranean plantings.

Do not pick leaves in the garden; just notice the culinary plant language.

Flowering shrub

Hydrangea / hortensia

Broad leaves and rounded flower heads; in heat they may look tired, but they are part of Bardini's named collection.

Best spotted where there is more shade and irrigation.

Insect

Honey bee

Small, amber-brown, flower-focused; usually too busy to care about you.

Watch flowering herbs, roses, and shaded blooms if anything is still active in the heat.

Insect

Violet carpenter bee

Large, dark, shiny, with violet-blue wings; looks dramatic, usually solitary.

Most likely around big flowers and warm walls if the garden still has nectar.

Insect

Swallowtail butterfly

Yellow wings, black veining, tail-like points; quick and elegant when it appears.

Not guaranteed, but plausible in warm gardens with fennel, rue, citrus, or nectar flowers nearby.

Reptile

Italian wall lizard

Small, fast, green-brown, sunning on stone, walls, steps, and dry edges.

Most likely in the warm margins of paths and masonry; blink and it has already judged your shoes.

Heat Desk

Today Is Not A Noon-Climb Day

  • Carry water and refill before crossing the river. A fan, hat, and electrolytes are not overkill.
  • Use taxis more generously than usual. The walk up toward Bardini, Rose Garden, or San Miniato is beautiful and also mean in heat.
  • Do not stack Boboli plus Bardini plus Rose Garden unless the evening is surprisingly forgiving. Pick one major garden and let it breathe.
  • For the cleanest day: Medici Chapels in the morning, rest in the afternoon, Bardini at golden hour, optional San Miniato stars.
