GLP-1 Texas Spa Cookbook

Original, home-cookable recipes inspired by wellness meals, juices, bowls, seafood dishes, and spa-cafe menus from leading Texas health resorts. Built for calm portions, protein-forward plates, gentle fiber, hydration, and guest-friendly pantry labeling.

Cookbook Rule

These are not proprietary resort recipes. They are Las Jaras versions inspired by public menus and wellness patterns: smaller plates, clean protein, easy digestion, and food that still feels like hospitality.

How To Use This Cookbook

For guests taking GLP-1 medicines, the meal strategy is small portions, protein first, gentle fiber, modest fat, and steady hydration. Each recipe includes a source inspiration, a meal description, pantry/fridge tags, and a health note. When a resort menu stated a benefit, that benefit is called out. When no benefit was stated, the note stays practical and non-medical.

Inspired by Lake Austin Spa Resort

Lovely Green Ginger Juice

A bright green juice with pineapple, cucumber, apple, lemon, ginger, and greens.

HydrationGentle stomach
  • 1 cup cucumber
  • 1/2 green apple
  • 1/2 cup pineapple
  • 1 packed cup spinach
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 1/2 cup cold water
  1. Blend everything until smooth.
  2. Strain for a spa-style juice, or leave whole for more fiber.
  3. Serve very cold in a small glass.

Stated benefit from source menu: hydrate, aid digestion, and support alkalizing balance.

Golden Aura Carrot Citrus Shot

Banana Oats Recovery Smoothie

A soft breakfast smoothie with banana, oats, almond milk, flax, cinnamon, and cocoa nibs.

Gentle fiberGentle stomach
  • 1 small banana
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 tablespoons rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon ground flax
  • 1 teaspoon honey, optional
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon cocoa nibs, optional
  1. Blend until fully smooth.
  2. Serve as one modest smoothie or two small portions.

Stated benefit from source menu: Quenche describes its juices and smoothies as nutrition-dense and wellness-supportive.

Egg White Kale Scramble

A light protein breakfast with mushrooms, tomato, greens, and a little goat cheese.

Protein-forwardSmall portion
  • 3 egg whites or 1/2 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1 cup chopped kale or spinach
  • 1/3 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup chopped tomato
  • 1 tablespoon goat cheese
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  1. Saute mushrooms and greens in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add egg whites and tomato; stir gently until set.
  3. Finish with goat cheese.

Stated benefit from source menu: gluten-free, vegetarian, and nut-free.

Autumn Grain Bowl With Avocado and Hemp

A small grain bowl with quinoa, sweet potato, cucumber, mushrooms, avocado, hemp seeds, and pesto.

Gentle fiberAdd protein
  • 1/3 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/3 cup roasted sweet potato cubes
  • 1/4 cup sauteed mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/4 avocado, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon hemp seeds
  • 1 tablespoon pesto
  • Optional: 3 ounces chicken, tuna, tofu, or salmon
  1. Arrange warm quinoa, sweet potato, and mushrooms in a bowl.
  2. Add cucumber, avocado, hemp, and pesto.
  3. Add protein if serving as a full meal.

Stated benefit from source menu: vegetarian and nut-free; no item-specific health claim.

Thai Lettuce Wraps With Tamari Chili Mushrooms

Butter lettuce cups filled with crunchy root slaw, marinated mushrooms, cilantro, and tamari-chili sauce.

Gentle fiberLight lunch

  • 1 cup shredded carrot and cabbage

  3. Top with cilantro and serve immediately.

Inspired by Miraval Austin

Achiote Citrus Chicken With Greens

Chicken with achiote-orange glaze, soft greens, and a small scoop of hominy grits or rice.

Protein-forwardDinner
  • 4 ounces chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 teaspoon achiote paste
  • 2 tablespoons orange juice
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice
  • 1 cup sauteed greens
  • 1/3 cup cooked hominy grits or rice
  1. Mix achiote, orange, lime, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Brush chicken and cook until done.
  3. Serve with greens and grits or rice.

Stated benefit from source menu: 38g protein listed; gluten-free, nut-free, soy-free.

Inspired by Miraval Austin

Fresh Catch With Turnip Puree

White fish with a light turnip puree, herbs, and a crisp potato bite.

Protein-forwardLight dinner
  • 4 ounces cod, snapper, or trout
  • 1 cup diced turnip
  • 1 small potato, grated or sliced thin
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Herbs, lemon, salt, and pepper
  1. Simmer turnip until soft; blend with a splash of cooking water and salt.
  2. Pan-sear fish in olive oil and lemon.
  3. Crisp the potato in a small nonstick pan.
  4. Plate fish over puree with potato and herbs.

Stated benefit from source menu: 36g protein listed; nut-free and soy-free.

Citrus Shrimp Ceviche Cups

A small chilled shrimp dish with lime, tomato, cucumber, cilantro, and avocado.

Protein-forwardHydration
  • 8 ounces cooked shrimp, chopped
  • 1/2 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/2 cup chopped cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon orange juice
  • 1/4 avocado, diced
  • Cilantro, salt, and jalapeno to taste
  1. Toss shrimp, tomato, cucumber, citrus, and cilantro.
  2. Chill 20 minutes.
  3. Fold in avocado just before serving.

Stated benefit from source menu: Ostra emphasizes sustainable seafood; no item-specific health claim.

Inspired by Hyatt Hill Country in-room dining

Blackened Redfish With Mango Cucumber Salsa

A lighter Texas fish plate with fruit salsa and a small rice portion.

Protein-forwardTexas resort dinner
  • 4 ounces redfish, snapper, or trout
  • 1/2 teaspoon blackening spice
  • 1/3 cup cooked rice
  • 1/4 cup diced mango
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumber
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  1. Season fish lightly with blackening spice.
  2. Sear in olive oil until cooked through.
  3. Mix mango, cucumber, lime, and salt.
  4. Serve fish with rice and salsa.

Stated benefit from source menu: none. Las Jaras note: lean fish, small rice portion, and water-rich salsa keep the plate lighter.

Las Jaras Pantry Labels

Use these labels on the pantry shelf or fridge bin instead of diet language. The label should help guests choose quickly without disclosing why they need a gentler option.

Hydration

Use for juices, mineral coolers, electrolyte packets, broth, herbal tea, sparkling water, watermelon salad, and cucumber-heavy snacks.

Protein-forward

Use for egg cups, Greek yogurt, chicken rice plates, fish plates, shrimp cups, tuna packets, cottage cheese, and edamame bowls.

Gentle Fiber

Use for oats, hummus, quinoa bowls, chia, flax, beans, lentils, applesauce, berries, and cooked vegetable soups.

Gentle Stomach

Use for tea, broth, rice, toast, crackers, banana smoothies, applesauce, and low-sauce plates.
