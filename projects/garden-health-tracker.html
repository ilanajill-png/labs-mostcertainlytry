    What the app tracks

    Identity: specimen ID, cultivar, location, planted date, source, acquisition cost, and multiple tag photos. Daily care: dated, plant-specific watering checklists and completion times. Growth: height, two-axis spread, stem diameter, phenology, vigor. Health: health score, soil moisture, leaf color, wilt, damage percentage, pests and symptoms. Inputs: water, rain, fertilizer, amendment, treatment. Yield: harvest count and weight. Evidence: multiple plant photos, filenames, capture date/time, save timestamp, symptom detail, notes, and next action.
