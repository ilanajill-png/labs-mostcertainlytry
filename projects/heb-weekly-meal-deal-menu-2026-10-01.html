Recipe 1 · First night

Mixed Fajita Supper With Beans And Queso

Serve the deal plainly once, while the tortillas and meats are at their best.

Fiesta plating

Fan tortillas in a towel-lined basket. Put chicken and beef in separate bands on a cobalt or bright platter; beans go in a small bowl so they do not muddy the meat.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz chicken fajitas
  • 4 oz beef fajitas
  • 6 corn tortillas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 2 oz queso quesadilla
  • Salsa, lime, cilantro, onion

Method

  1. Warm meats separately in a hot skillet so their edges brown.
  2. Cook tortillas according to the package.
  3. Warm beans with a spoonful of broth or water.
  4. Serve family-style with shredded queso and fresh finishers.

Drink pairing

Serve the free Mi Tienda refresco over ice with lime. The carbonation and citrus cut the richness of beef, queso, and beans.

Recipe inspiration: Easy Fajitas — H-E-B. Adapted for the cooked Mi Tienda fajitas and Meal Deal sides.

Recipe 2 · Fast skillet

Mixed Fajita Quesadillas

The free queso does the structural work; salsa stays outside so the tortillas remain crisp.

Fiesta plating

Cut each quesadilla into three wedges and stack them slightly offset. Use salsa as a bright pool or side cup, not a blanket.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 3 oz chicken fajitas
  • 2 oz beef fajitas
  • 3 oz queso quesadilla
  • Onion, cilantro, salsa, lime

Method

  1. Chop and warm the meat.
  2. Layer queso and meat between tortillas.
  3. Cook in a dry skillet until crisp and fully melted, flipping once.
  4. Rest one minute, cut, and finish with lime.

Plate it

Add a cabbage-lime slaw if you bought cabbage; it gives the plate height and acidity without requiring crema.

Recipe inspiration: Fajita Quesadilla — H-E-B. Adapted for mixed cooked fajitas and the free queso quesadilla.

Recipe 3 · Pantry bowl

Beef Fajita Quinoa Bowls

Use the existing tri-color quinoa rather than buying rice to duplicate the deal’s starch option.

Fiesta plating

Quinoa fills half the bowl. Arrange beef, beans, salsa, and avocado or cabbage in separate arcs; finish with queso at the center.

Ingredients

  • 1 bag cooked tri-color quinoa
  • 5 oz beef fajitas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 oz queso
  • Salsa, lime, cilantro
  • Optional avocado or cabbage

Method

  1. Cook quinoa as directed; season with lime and cilantro.
  2. Crisp beef in a skillet.
  3. Warm beans with enough water to spoon easily.
  4. Build bowls and add cold toppings last.

Recipe inspiration: Grilled Fajita Chicken Bowl — H-E-B. Adapted with beef, pantry quinoa, and the Meal Deal beans.

Recipe 4 · Soup night

Chicken Fajita Fideo

Mi Tienda estrellas or letras pasta and stocked broth turn a small meat portion into a real soup.

Fiesta plating

Use a deep bowl. Keep tortilla strips high and dry at the center; cilantro and lime go on at the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz chicken fajitas, chopped
  • 1 cup small pasta
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • ¼ cup refried beans
  • 2 tortillas, cut into strips
  • Salsa or wet onion-oregano seasoning

Method

  1. Toast pasta lightly in oil if available.
  2. Add broth and a spoonful of salsa or seasoning; simmer until pasta is tender.
  3. Whisk in beans for body, then add chicken just to heat.
  4. Toast tortilla strips in a dry skillet and scatter over bowls.

Recipe inspiration: Easy Fideo with Chicken — H-E-B. Adapted for cooked fajita chicken, small pantry pasta, and tortilla strips.

Recipe 5 · Masa night

Fajita Masa Cakes With Beans And Queso

Mi Tienda masa makes the week feel less like reheating and more like a second cuisine.

Fiesta plating

Serve three small cakes per plate in a triangle. Keep toppings centered so the crisp brown edges remain visible.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Mi Tienda masa mix
  • Water and salt as directed
  • 4 oz mixed fajita meat
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 oz queso
  • Salsa, cilantro, onion

Method

  1. Mix masa, form six small thick disks, and pan-cook until browned and cooked through.
  2. Warm beans and meat separately.
  3. Spread each cake with beans, then add meat, queso, salsa, and herbs.

Recipe inspiration: Mexican Sopes — Isabel Eats. The masa-cake technique is adapted for Mi Tienda masa and the Meal Deal toppings.

Recipe 6 · Crunch night

Baked Fajita Tostadas

The free tortillas become their own chips, avoiding another bagged purchase.

Fiesta plating

Use two tostadas per plate with a lime wedge between them. Contrast crisp tan tortillas with green cilantro and red salsa.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 4 oz chicken or beef fajitas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1½ oz queso
  • Salsa, cabbage, cilantro, lime

Method

  1. Brush or spray tortillas lightly with oil; bake at 400°F until crisp, flipping once.
  2. Spread with hot beans, then top with meat and queso.
  3. Return to the oven just until melted; add salsa and fresh crunch.

Recipe inspiration: Bean and Chicken Tostadas — H-E-B. Adapted to bake the Meal Deal tortillas and use either fajita meat.

Recipe 7 · Brunch save

Fajita Chilaquiles Skillet

Use tortillas that are drying out; eggs are optional, not required for the base meal.

Fiesta plating

Serve directly from the skillet or in shallow bowls. Keep queso and onion on top so the softened tortillas still show.

Ingredients

  • 4 tortillas, cut into wedges
  • 4 oz mixed fajita meat
  • 1 cup salsa plus water as needed
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 oz queso
  • Optional 2–4 eggs

Method

  1. Toast tortilla wedges until crisp.
  2. Warm salsa with a splash of water, then fold in wedges briefly.
  3. Add hot meat, spoonfuls of beans, and queso.
  4. Top with fried eggs only if using.

Recipe inspiration: Chicken Chilaquiles — H-E-B. Adapted for mixed cooked fajitas and optional eggs.

Recipe 8 · Clean-out

End-of-Deal Fajita Tortilla Soup

The intentionally flexible final meal catches whatever remains without disguising it as scraps.

Fiesta plating

Use the largest bowls. Put queso beneath the hot soup so it softens; add tortilla strips and herbs only at serving.

Ingredients

  • All remaining fajita meat
  • Remaining refried beans
  • 4 cups chicken or beef broth
  • Remaining tortillas, cut into strips
  • Salsa or tomato sauce
  • Remaining queso and fresh toppings

Method

  1. Simmer broth with salsa or a little tomato sauce.
  2. Whisk in beans to thicken lightly.
  3. Add chopped meat just long enough to heat.
  4. Toast tortilla strips and serve over bowls with the last queso and herbs.

Recipe inspiration: Ultimate Tortilla Soup — H-E-B. Adapted as a flexible clean-out soup for the remaining Meal Deal ingredients.
