Recipe 1 · First night

Mixed Fajita Supper With Beans And Queso

Serve the deal plainly once, while the tortillas and meats are at their best.

Fiesta plating

Fan tortillas in a towel-lined basket. Put chicken and beef in separate bands on a cobalt or bright platter; beans go in a small bowl so they do not muddy the meat.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz chicken fajitas
  • 4 oz beef fajitas
  • 1 small onion, cut into ½-inch rings
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into ½-inch rings
  • 1 tsp. neutral oil
  • ½ tsp. red chile powder
  • Pinch each sugar and kosher salt
  • 1½ Tbsp. unsalted butter
  • Zest of ½ lime
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 4 corn tortillas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • Salsa, guacamole or avocado, crema, lettuce, and lime wedges

Method

  1. Stir the chile powder, sugar, and salt together. Toss the onion and pepper with the oil and seasoning.
  2. Melt the butter; stir in the lime zest and grated garlic. Reserve.
  3. Char the onion and pepper in a very hot cast-iron skillet or grill pan, turning once, until lightly blistered. Toss with one-third of the lime-garlic butter and keep warm.
  4. Reheat the cooked chicken and beef separately just until hot and browned at the edges. Do not simmer or marinate the cooked meat. Brush with another third of the butter.
  5. Heat the empty cast-iron skillet until very hot. Add vegetables, top with meat, and drizzle the remaining butter around the exposed skillet edge for the source recipe’s sizzling presentation.
  6. Serve immediately with warm tortillas, beans, salsa, guacamole, crema, lettuce, and lime.

Drink pairing

Serve the free Mi Tienda refresco over ice with lime. The carbonation and citrus cut the richness of beef, queso, and beans.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Steak Fajitas — Bon Appétit. This version keeps the chile-seasoned charred vegetables, lime-garlic butter, cast-iron sizzle, and full serving setup. Only the raw-steak marinade and grilling steps are omitted because both Meal Deal meats arrive fully cooked.

Recipe 2 · Fast skillet

Mixed Fajita Quesadillas

The free queso does the structural work; salsa stays outside so the tortillas remain crisp.

Fiesta plating

Cut each quesadilla into three wedges and stack them slightly offset. Use salsa as a bright pool or side cup, not a blanket.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 5 oz chicken fajitas, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • ¼ tsp. each paprika, ground coriander, and ground cumin
  • Pinch of sugar
  • 3 oz queso quesadilla, coarsely grated
  • ¼ small onion, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • ¼ red bell pepper, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • ½ garlic clove, finely chopped
  • Jalapeño to taste, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp. coarsely chopped cilantro
  • 1–2 tsp. neutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the cooked chicken with paprika, coriander, cumin, and sugar.
  2. Sauté the onion and pepper in a little oil over medium-high until translucent and lightly charred, about 4 minutes. Add garlic and jalapeño and cook 30 seconds.
  3. Add chicken and toss only until warmed. Transfer to a bowl and mix with the grated queso and cilantro.
  4. Divide the filling among the tortillas, covering one half of each; fold the empty halves over.
  5. Cook in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat, turning halfway through, until the tortillas are golden and the cheese is melted, about 5 minutes total. Cut into wedges and serve.

Plate it

Add a cabbage-lime slaw if you bought cabbage; it gives the plate height and acidity without requiring crema.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chicken Quesadillas — Bon Appétit. The paprika-coriander-cumin seasoning, pepper-onion-garlic-jalapeño filling, cheese-to-chicken structure, cilantro, folding, and oiled-skillet method are retained. Cooked chicken fajitas replace the source’s raw chicken breasts; the free corn tortillas replace flour tortillas.

Recipe 3 · Pantry bowl

Beef Fajita Quinoa Bowls

Use the existing tri-color quinoa rather than buying rice to duplicate the deal’s starch option.

Fiesta plating

Quinoa fills half the bowl. Arrange beef, beans, salsa, and avocado or cabbage in separate arcs; finish with queso at the center.

Ingredients

  • Cooked tri-color quinoa for 2
  • 5 oz beef fajitas, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • ¼ red onion, finely chopped
  • ½ jalapeño, divided
  • ¼ cup finely chopped cilantro
  • 2 Tbsp. lime juice
  • ¼ small yellow onion, coarsely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, coarsely chopped
  • 1 tsp. olive oil
  • 1 tsp. taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • Avocado, shredded lettuce, and lime wedges

Method

  1. Make the source recipe’s salsa: combine tomato, red onion, half the jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice, and salt.
  2. Finely chop or pulse the remaining jalapeño, yellow onion, and garlic into a coarse paste.
  3. Heat the oil over medium-high. Cook the onion mixture with the taco seasoning until fragrant and beginning to stick, 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the chopped cooked fajita beef and ¼ cup water. Scrape the pan and cook just until the sauce thickens and coats the beef, about 2 minutes.
  5. Divide quinoa and beef among bowls. Serve with avocado, lettuce, lime wedges, and the reserved fresh salsa.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Easy Taco Bowls — Bon Appétit. The fresh salsa, jalapeño-onion-garlic sofrito, taco-seasoned pan sauce, avocado, lettuce, lime, and composed-bowl structure are retained. The source explicitly permits quinoa in place of rice; chopped cooked fajita beef replaces ground beef.

Recipe 4 · Soup night

Chicken Fajita Fideo

Mi Tienda estrellas or letras pasta and stocked broth turn a small meat portion into a real soup.

Fiesta plating

Use a deep bowl. Float diced avocado and crumbled queso at the center; cilantro and lime go on at the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz chicken fajitas, chopped
  • 4 oz fideo, vermicelli, or small pasta
  • 5 oz plum tomatoes, chopped
  • ¼ cup white onion, chopped
  • ½ large garlic clove, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp. neutral oil
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • Salt and pepper
  • Crumbled queso and diced avocado

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, onion, and garlic until smooth, then strain into a bowl.
  2. Heat the oil over medium-low. Fry the dry noodles, stirring often, until light golden brown, 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato mixture and cook 1 minute. Stir in the broth and bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until the noodles are just tender, about 8 minutes; do not overcook.
  5. Add the cooked chicken only for the final minute. Season with salt and pepper; garnish with queso and avocado.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Sopa de Fideo — Bon Appétit, excerpted there from Mely Martínez’s The Mexican Home Kitchen. The ingredient ratio, blended-and-strained tomato base, fried noodles, covered simmer, and queso-avocado garnish are retained at half scale. Cooked chicken fajitas are the sole addition.

Recipe 5 · Oven night

Chicken Fajita Enchiladas Verdes

Roasted tomatillos, poblano, serrano, cilantro, and lime make the source recipe’s green sauce; cooked chicken fajitas shorten only the filling step.

Fiesta plating

Spoon sauce under each enchilada, then finish with a thin crema ribbon and red-onion curls. Keep the browned queso visible.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp. olive oil
  • ¼ onion
  • ½ poblano chile
  • 1 lb. tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 1 serrano chile, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, smashed
  • ½ cup chicken broth
  • ¼ cup cilantro leaves and tender stems
  • 1½ tsp. lime juice
  • 4 oz chicken fajitas, shredded or finely chopped
  • ¼ tsp. ground coriander
  • Pinch chile de árbol or red-pepper flakes
  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 3 oz queso quesadilla, grated
  • 2 Tbsp. crema or plain yogurt loosened with milk
  • Thinly sliced red onion

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Drizzle the olive oil over onion, poblano, and tomatillos on a rimmed sheet pan. Roast until soft and browned, 35–40 minutes; cool slightly and peel the poblano.
  2. Blend the roasted vegetables and pan juices with serrano, garlic, broth, cilantro, and lime until smooth. Season with salt.
  3. Toss the cooked chicken with coriander, chile de árbol, and ¼ cup green sauce.
  4. Lightly fry each tortilla in a thin film of oil for about 10 seconds per side so it will roll without tearing. Dip both sides in green sauce.
  5. Spread sauce in a small baking dish. Fill each tortilla with chicken, roll, and arrange seam-side down. Top with remaining sauce and queso.
  6. Bake until bubbling and beginning to brown, 20–25 minutes; rest 10 minutes. Finish with crema and sliced red onion.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chicken Enchiladas — Bon Appétit. This half-scale version retains the roasted tomatillo-poblano sauce, seasoned chicken filling, briefly fried and sauce-dipped tortillas, rolled assembly, baked cheese, crema, and red onion. Chicken fajitas replace rotisserie chicken; the free queso quesadilla replaces Cotija.

Recipe 6 · Crunch night

Baked Fajita Tostadas

The free tortillas become their own chips, avoiding another bagged purchase.

Fiesta plating

Use two tostadas per plate with a lime wedge between them. Contrast crisp tan tortillas with green cilantro and red salsa.

Ingredients

  • 2 corn tortillas
  • 4 oz beef fajitas, finely chopped
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp. white onion, finely chopped
  • Jalapeño and cilantro to taste
  • Juice of ¼ lime
  • ½ tsp. olive oil
  • Pinches of cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, and cayenne
  • ½ garlic clove, thinly sliced
  • 1½ tsp. tomato paste
  • 2 Tbsp. water
  • ½ avocado, thinly sliced
  • 1 oz queso, crumbled or grated

Method

  1. Make pico de gallo with tomato, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  2. Brush tortillas lightly with oil and bake at 400°F, flipping once, until rigid and crisp.
  3. Heat olive oil over medium-high. Add chopped cooked beef with cumin, coriander, paprika, and cayenne and cook just until browned at the edges.
  4. Add sliced garlic and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes. Add water and stir until a glossy sauce coats the beef.
  5. Warm the beans. Layer each tostada with beans, spiced beef, pico, avocado, and queso. Serve immediately.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Spiced Beef and Refried Bean Tostadas — Bon Appétit. The pico, cumin-coriander-paprika-cayenne seasoning, garlic-and-tomato-paste pan sauce, refried-bean layer, avocado, and cheese are retained. Chopped cooked fajita beef replaces ground beef; two free tortillas are oven-crisped in place of packaged tostadas.

Recipe 7 · Brunch save

Fajita Chilaquiles Skillet

Use tortillas that are drying out; eggs are optional, not required for the base meal.

Fiesta plating

Serve directly from the skillet or in shallow bowls. Keep queso and onion on top so the softened tortillas still show.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas, cut into 6 wedges each
  • Neutral oil and kosher salt
  • 4 oz mixed fajita meat
  • 1½ cups homemade salsa roja, divided
  • For the salsa: ¼ white onion, 1 plum tomato, ½ serrano, 1 garlic clove, ¾ oz. dried ancho/guajillo/pasilla chiles, 2 Tbsp. cilantro, juice of ¼ lime, pinch each cumin and cinnamon
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • 1 oz queso, crumbled
  • 1 Tbsp. crema
  • Cilantro and lime wedges

Method

  1. For the salsa, cover onion, tomato, serrano, garlic, and dried chiles with water. Bring to a boil, remove from heat, cover, and steep 20–25 minutes.
  2. Transfer the softened vegetables and chiles to a blender with cilantro, lime, cumin, cinnamon, salt, and enough soaking liquid to blend smooth.
  3. Lightly oil and bake or shallow-fry the tortilla wedges until sturdy, crisp, and golden; salt while hot.
  4. Warm the cooked meat with ¼ cup salsa in a small pan for about 5 minutes.
  5. Bring the remaining salsa to a simmer in a wide skillet. Add the crisp wedges and toss to coat. Cover, remove from heat, and rest 1 minute for crunchy chips, 3 minutes for some crunch, or 5 minutes for soft chilaquiles.
  6. Plate immediately with hot meat, avocado, queso, crema, cilantro, and lime.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chilaquiles Rojos — Bon Appétit, using its linked Salsa Roja — Epicurious. This version retains homemade chile salsa, crisp tortilla wedges, meat warmed separately with salsa, the source’s timed soak, and the avocado-queso-crema finish. Meal Deal fajitas replace roast chicken or carnitas.

Recipe 8 · Clean-out

End-of-Deal Fajita Tortilla Soup

The intentionally flexible final meal catches whatever remains without disguising it as scraps.

Fiesta plating

Use the largest bowls. Put queso beneath the hot soup so it softens; add tortilla strips and herbs only at serving.

Ingredients

  • All remaining fajita meat
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • ⅓ white onion
  • 4 cups chicken or beef broth
  • Remaining tortillas, cut into thin strips
  • 1 pasilla chile, sliced into rings, optional
  • Epazote, cilantro, or both
  • Remaining queso, avocado, crema, and lime

Method

  1. Dry-roast the tomatoes, unpeeled garlic, and onion in a skillet until blistered. Peel the garlic and tomatoes, then blend the vegetables smooth with a little broth.
  2. Cook the purée in a spoonful of oil until darker and reduced, about 5–8 minutes. Add the remaining broth and simmer 10 minutes; add epazote if using.
  3. Crisp the tortilla strips in a skillet with oil; quickly crisp the pasilla rings separately, watching closely.
  4. Add the chopped cooked meat to the soup only long enough to heat. Season to taste.
  5. Put queso beneath the hot soup; finish with tortilla strips, pasilla, avocado, crema, herbs, and lime. Serve leftover beans on the side rather than muddying the tomato broth.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Tortilla Soup — Bon Appétit, excerpted there from Mely Martínez’s The Mexican Home Kitchen. The dry-roasted vegetables, reduced tomato purée, epazote broth, separately crisped tortilla and pasilla garnishes, avocado, cheese, crema, and lime are retained. Remaining cooked fajitas replace the source’s optional shredded chicken.
