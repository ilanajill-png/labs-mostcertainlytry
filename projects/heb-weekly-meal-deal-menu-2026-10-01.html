Recipe 1 · First night

El Paso–Inspired Steak Fajitas & Vegetables

Bon Appétit’s charred-vegetable and lime-garlic-butter method, paired with H‑E‑B’s fully cooked Mi Tienda beef fajitas.

Fiesta plating

Serve the sizzling skillet at the table with warm tortillas and the garnishes in separate bowls so everyone can build fajitas to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 package (16 oz) H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Beef for Fajitas
  • 1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal kosher salt
  • 1 Tbsp. H‑E‑B ancho-guajillo chile powder
  • 1 tsp. H‑E‑B granulated sugar
  • 6 Tbsp. H‑E‑B unsalted butter
  • Zest of 2 fresh limes
  • 2 cloves fresh garlic, finely grated
  • 2 large yellow onions, sliced into ½-inch-thick rings
  • 2 large red bell peppers, ribs and seeds removed, sliced into ½-inch-thick rings
  • 4½ tsp., plus 1 Tbsp. and more for the grate, vegetable oil
  • 4 H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas, warmed
  • H‑E‑B fresh salsa, for serving
  • H‑E‑B fresh guacamole, for serving
  • Mi Tienda crema agria, for serving
  • H‑E‑B shredded iceberg lettuce, for serving
  • Fresh lime wedges, for serving
  • H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frijoles Pintos Refritos, for serving alongside

Method

  1. Mix the vegetable seasoning. Mix 1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt, 1 Tbsp. red chile powder, and 1 tsp. granulated sugar in a large heatproof bowl to combine.
  2. Make the lime-garlic butter. Prepare a grill for high heat; oil grate. Melt 6 Tbsp. unsalted butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Remove from heat and stir in the zest of 2 limes and 2 garlic cloves, finely grated.
  3. Grill the vegetables. Add 2 large onions, sliced into ½-inch-thick rings, 2 large red bell peppers, ribs and seeds removed and sliced into ½-inch-thick rings, and 4½ tsp. vegetable oil to the remaining salt mixture in the bowl and toss to coat. Transfer onions and bell peppers to the grate and grill, turning halfway through, until lightly charred, 2–4 minutes. Return to the bowl and drizzle about 2 Tbsp. lime-garlic butter over; toss to coat. Cover and keep warm until ready to use.
  4. Heat the H‑E‑B beef fajitas. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Pour 1 Tbsp. oil into the pan and heat for 30–60 seconds. Place the frozen strips into the heated, oiled skillet in a single layer. Cover skillet and heat on medium for 6–8 minutes, or until heated thoroughly. Flip the meat during heating to prevent burning. Transfer steak to a clean baking sheet and brush both sides with some lime-garlic butter. Tent with foil and let rest about 5 minutes.
  5. Assemble and serve. Heat a dry large cast-iron skillet over high until very hot. Slice steak against the grain ½ inch thick. Transfer vegetables to the skillet and top with steak. Drizzle remaining lime-garlic butter around the edges; it will sizzle and steam. Serve with warm tortillas, salsa, guacamole, sour cream, shredded iceberg lettuce, and lime wedges for building fajitas.

Drink pairing · El Paso Refresco Highball

Rim a tall glass with equal parts Tajín Clásico and kosher salt, then fill it with ice. Add 1 oz blanco tequila, ½ oz mezcal, and ½ oz juice from a fresh lime. Top slowly with 3–4 oz chilled H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Limón Refresco, stir once, and garnish with a lime wedge. H‑E‑B does not sell distilled spirits in Texas, so tequila and mezcal require a licensed liquor retailer.

Cocktail inspiration: Mezcal Paloma — Bon Appétit. The tequila-mezcal, lime, salt, and tall sparkling format are retained; sweet Mi Tienda refresco replaces the source recipe’s citrus and soda components.

Recipe 2 · Fast skillet

Chicken Fajita Quesadillas

The free queso does the structural work; salsa stays outside so the tortillas remain crisp.

Fiesta plating

Cut each quesadilla into three wedges and stack them slightly offset. Use salsa as a bright pool or side cup, not a blanket.

Ingredients

  • 4 H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas
  • 5 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • 3 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Queso Quesadilla, coarsely grated
  • ¼ small yellow onion, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • ¼ red bell pepper, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • ½ clove fresh garlic, finely chopped
  • Fresh jalapeño to taste, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp. coarsely chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1–2 tsp. vegetable oil
  • H‑E‑B fresh salsa, for serving

Method

  1. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook the onion and bell pepper, stirring often, until lightly charred in spots and the onion is translucent, about 4 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and jalapeño and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Transfer the vegetables to a bowl.
  3. Warm the fully cooked chicken fajitas according to their package directions, cut into ½-inch pieces, and add them to the vegetables. Mix in the grated queso and cilantro.
  4. Divide the filling among the tortillas, covering one half of each; fold the empty halves over.
  5. Cook in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat, turning halfway through, until the tortillas are golden and the cheese is melted, about 5 minutes total. Cut into wedges and serve.

Plate it

Add a cabbage-lime slaw if you bought cabbage; it gives the plate height and acidity without requiring crema.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chicken Quesadillas — Bon Appétit. The source’s pepper–onion–garlic–jalapeño cooking sequence, cheese, cilantro, folding, and oiled-skillet method are retained. Its raw-chicken slicing, spice-rub, and cooking steps are omitted because the Meal Deal chicken arrives seasoned and fully cooked; it is warmed according to the package before being added to the source’s vegetable mixture.

Recipe 3 · Quinoa bowl

Chicken Fajita Quinoa Bowls

Tri-color quinoa replaces rice while preserving the source recipe’s composed-bowl structure.

Fiesta plating

Quinoa fills half the bowl. Arrange chicken, salsa, avocado, and lettuce in separate arcs.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry tri-color quinoa, cooked according to package directions
  • 5 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas, finely chopped
  • 1 Roma tomato, chopped
  • ¼ red onion, finely chopped
  • ½ fresh jalapeño, divided
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 Tbsp. juice from fresh limes
  • ¼ small yellow onion, coarsely chopped
  • 1 clove fresh garlic, coarsely chopped
  • 1 tsp. H‑E‑B olive oil
  • 1 tsp. H‑E‑B taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • 1 large Hass avocado, sliced
  • H‑E‑B shredded iceberg lettuce, for serving
  • Fresh lime wedges, for serving

Method

  1. Make the source recipe’s salsa: combine tomato, red onion, half the jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice, and salt.
  2. Finely chop or pulse the remaining jalapeño, yellow onion, and garlic into a coarse paste.
  3. Heat the oil over medium-high. Cook the onion mixture with the taco seasoning until fragrant and beginning to stick, 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the chopped cooked fajita chicken and ¼ cup water. Scrape the pan and cook just until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken, about 2 minutes.
  5. Divide quinoa and chicken among bowls. Serve with avocado, lettuce, lime wedges, and the reserved fresh salsa.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Easy Taco Bowls — Bon Appétit. The fresh salsa, jalapeño-onion-garlic sofrito, taco-seasoned pan sauce, avocado, lettuce, lime, and composed-bowl structure are retained. The source explicitly permits quinoa in place of rice; chopped cooked chicken fajitas replace ground beef.

Recipe 4 · Soup night

Chicken Fajita Fideo

Mi Tienda estrellas pasta and chicken broth turn a small meat portion into a real soup.

Fiesta plating

Use a deep bowl. Float diced avocado and crumbled queso at the center; cilantro and lime go on at the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas, chopped
  • 4 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Estrellas Star Pasta
  • 5 oz Roma tomatoes, chopped
  • ¼ cup yellow onion, chopped
  • ½ large clove fresh garlic, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil
  • 3 cups H‑E‑B chicken broth
  • Kosher salt and H‑E‑B ground black pepper
  • Mi Tienda queso quesadilla, crumbled, and 1 diced large Hass avocado
  • Fresh cilantro and fresh lime wedges, for serving

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, onion, and garlic until smooth, then strain into a bowl.
  2. Heat the oil over medium-low. Fry the dry noodles, stirring often, until light golden brown, 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato mixture and cook 1 minute. Stir in the broth and bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until the noodles are just tender, about 8 minutes; do not overcook.
  5. Add the cooked chicken only for the final minute. Season with salt and pepper; garnish with queso and avocado.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Sopa de Fideo — Bon Appétit, excerpted there from Mely Martínez’s The Mexican Home Kitchen. The ingredient ratio, blended-and-strained tomato base, fried noodles, covered simmer, and queso-avocado garnish are retained at half scale. Cooked chicken fajitas are the sole addition.

Recipe 5 · Oven night

Chicken Fajita Enchiladas Verdes

Roasted tomatillos, poblano, serrano, cilantro, and lime make the source recipe’s green sauce; cooked chicken fajitas shorten only the filling step.

Fiesta plating

Spoon sauce under each enchilada, then finish with a thin crema ribbon and red-onion curls. Keep the browned queso visible.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp. H‑E‑B olive oil
  • ¼ yellow onion
  • ½ fresh poblano pepper
  • 1 lb. fresh tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 1 fresh serrano pepper, chopped
  • 1 clove fresh garlic, smashed
  • ½ cup H‑E‑B chicken broth
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems
  • 1½ tsp. juice from a fresh lime
  • 4 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas, shredded or finely chopped
  • ¼ tsp. ground coriander
  • Pinch H‑E‑B crushed red pepper
  • 4 H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas
  • 3 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Queso Quesadilla, grated
  • 2 Tbsp. H‑E‑B Mi Tienda crema agria
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • Thinly sliced fresh red onion

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Drizzle the olive oil over onion, poblano, and tomatillos on a rimmed sheet pan. Roast until soft and browned, 35–40 minutes; cool slightly and peel the poblano.
  2. Blend the roasted vegetables and pan juices with serrano, garlic, broth, cilantro, and lime until smooth. Season with salt.
  3. Toss the cooked chicken with coriander, chile de árbol, and ¼ cup green sauce.
  4. Lightly fry each tortilla in a thin film of oil for about 10 seconds per side so it will roll without tearing. Dip both sides in green sauce.
  5. Spread sauce in a small baking dish. Fill each tortilla with chicken, roll, and arrange seam-side down. Top with remaining sauce and queso.
  6. Bake until bubbling and beginning to brown, 20–25 minutes; rest 10 minutes. Finish with crema and sliced red onion.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chicken Enchiladas — Bon Appétit. This half-scale version retains the roasted tomatillo-poblano sauce, seasoned chicken filling, briefly fried and sauce-dipped tortillas, rolled assembly, baked cheese, crema, and red onion. Chicken fajitas replace rotisserie chicken; the free queso quesadilla replaces Cotija.

Recipe 6 · Crunch night

Baked Fajita Tostadas

The free tortillas become their own chips, avoiding another bagged purchase.

Fiesta plating

Use two tostadas per plate with a lime wedge between them. Contrast crisp tan tortillas with green cilantro and fresh red pico de gallo.

Ingredients

  • 2 H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas
  • 4 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas, finely chopped
  • ½ cup H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frijoles Pintos Refritos
  • 1 small Roma tomato, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp. yellow onion, finely chopped
  • Fresh jalapeño and fresh cilantro, to taste
  • Juice of ¼ fresh lime
  • ½ tsp. H‑E‑B olive oil
  • Pinches of ground cumin, ground coriander, smoked paprika, and cayenne
  • ½ clove fresh garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1½ tsp. H‑E‑B tomato paste
  • 2 Tbsp. water
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • ½ large Hass avocado, thinly sliced
  • 1 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Queso Quesadilla, crumbled or grated

Method

  1. Make pico de gallo with tomato, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  2. Brush tortillas lightly with oil and bake at 400°F, flipping once, until rigid and crisp.
  3. Heat olive oil over medium-high. Add chopped cooked chicken with cumin, coriander, paprika, and cayenne and cook just until browned at the edges.
  4. Add sliced garlic and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes. Add water and stir until a glossy sauce coats the chicken.
  5. Warm the beans. Layer each tostada with beans, spiced chicken, pico, avocado, and queso. Serve immediately.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Spiced Beef and Refried Bean Tostadas — Bon Appétit. The pico, cumin-coriander-paprika-cayenne seasoning, garlic-and-tomato-paste pan sauce, refried-bean layer, avocado, and cheese are retained. Chopped cooked chicken fajitas replace ground beef; two free tortillas are oven-crisped in place of packaged tostadas.

Recipe 7 · Brunch save

Fajita Chilaquiles Skillet

Use tortillas that are drying out; eggs are optional, not required for the base meal.

Fiesta plating

Serve directly from the skillet or in shallow bowls. Keep queso and onion on top so the softened tortillas still show.

Ingredients

  • 4 H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas, cut into 6 wedges each
  • Vegetable oil and kosher salt
  • 4 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas
  • 1½ cups homemade salsa roja, made with the linked ingredients below
  • ¼ yellow onion
  • 1 Roma tomato
  • ½ fresh serrano pepper
  • 1 clove fresh garlic
  • ¾ oz. Mi Tienda dried guajillo chiles
  • 2 Tbsp. fresh cilantro
  • Juice of ¼ fresh lime
  • Pinch each ground cumin and ground cinnamon
  • ½ large Hass avocado, sliced
  • 1 oz H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Queso Quesadilla, crumbled
  • 1 Tbsp. H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Crema Agria
  • Fresh cilantro and fresh lime wedges, for serving

Method

  1. For the salsa, cover onion, tomato, serrano, garlic, and dried chiles with water. Bring to a boil, remove from heat, cover, and steep 20–25 minutes.
  2. Transfer the softened vegetables and chiles to a blender with cilantro, lime, cumin, cinnamon, salt, and enough soaking liquid to blend smooth.
  3. Lightly oil and bake or shallow-fry the tortilla wedges until sturdy, crisp, and golden; salt while hot.
  4. Warm the cooked meat with ¼ cup salsa in a small pan for about 5 minutes.
  5. Bring the remaining salsa to a simmer in a wide skillet. Add the crisp wedges and toss to coat. Cover, remove from heat, and rest 1 minute for crunchy chips, 3 minutes for some crunch, or 5 minutes for soft chilaquiles.
  6. Plate immediately with hot meat, avocado, queso, crema, cilantro, and lime.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Chilaquiles Rojos — Bon Appétit, using its linked Salsa Roja — Epicurious. This version retains homemade chile salsa, crisp tortilla wedges, meat warmed separately with salsa, the source’s timed soak, and the avocado-queso-crema finish. Meal Deal chicken fajitas replace roast chicken or carnitas.

Recipe 8 · Clean-out

End-of-Deal Fajita Tortilla Soup

The intentionally flexible final meal catches whatever remains without disguising it as scraps.

Fiesta plating

Use the largest bowls. Put queso beneath the hot soup so it softens; add tortilla strips and herbs only at serving.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz remaining H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Frozen Fully Cooked Seasoned Chicken Thighs for Fajitas
  • 2 medium Roma tomatoes
  • 2 cloves fresh garlic, unpeeled
  • ⅓ yellow onion
  • 4 cups H‑E‑B chicken broth
  • 2 Tbsp. vegetable oil, divided
  • Remaining H‑E‑B Mi Tienda Ready to Cook Yellow Corn Tortillas, cut into thin strips
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Remaining Mi Tienda queso quesadilla
  • 1 large Hass avocado
  • Remaining Mi Tienda crema agria
  • Kosher salt and H‑E‑B ground black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh lime wedges

Method

  1. Dry-roast the tomatoes, unpeeled garlic, and onion in a skillet until blistered. Peel the garlic and tomatoes, then blend the vegetables smooth with a little broth.
  2. Cook the purée in 1 Tbsp. oil until darker and reduced, about 5–8 minutes. Add the remaining broth and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Crisp the tortilla strips in the remaining 1 Tbsp. oil.
  4. Add the chopped cooked chicken to the soup only long enough to heat. Season to taste.
  5. Put queso beneath the hot soup; finish with tortilla strips, avocado, crema, cilantro, and lime. Serve leftover beans on the side rather than muddying the tomato broth.

Meal Deal adaptation of: Tortilla Soup — Bon Appétit, excerpted there from Mely Martínez’s The Mexican Home Kitchen. The dry-roasted vegetables, reduced tomato purée, crisp tortilla garnish, avocado, cheese, crema, and lime are retained. Remaining cooked chicken fajitas replace the source’s optional shredded chicken; cilantro replaces hard-to-source fresh epazote.
