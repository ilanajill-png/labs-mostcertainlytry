Recipe 1 · First night

Mixed Fajita Supper With Beans And Queso

Serve the deal plainly once, while the tortillas and meats are at their best.

Fiesta plating

Fan tortillas in a towel-lined basket. Put chicken and beef in separate bands on a cobalt or bright platter; beans go in a small bowl so they do not muddy the meat.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz chicken fajitas
  • 4 oz beef fajitas
  • 1 small onion, cut into thick rings
  • 1 bell pepper, cut into thick strips
  • 1 Tbsp. butter or oil
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • Zest and juice of ½ lime
  • 6 corn tortillas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 2 oz queso quesadilla
  • Salsa, cilantro, and lime wedges

Method

  1. Mix the butter or oil with grated garlic and lime zest.
  2. Char the onion and pepper in a very hot skillet; toss with half the lime-garlic mixture and keep warm.
  3. Reheat the cooked chicken and beef separately in the same skillet just until their edges brown. Finish with the remaining lime-garlic mixture and a squeeze of lime.
  4. Warm the tortillas and beans. Serve everything family-style with queso, salsa, cilantro, and lime wedges.

Drink pairing

Serve the free Mi Tienda refresco over ice with lime. The carbonation and citrus cut the richness of beef, queso, and beans.

Editorial source reviewed: Steak Fajitas — Bon Appétit. The source’s charred vegetables and lime-garlic butter are retained; its raw-steak marinade is omitted because the Meal Deal fajitas are already cooked.

Recipe 2 · Fast skillet

Mixed Fajita Quesadillas

The free queso does the structural work; salsa stays outside so the tortillas remain crisp.

Fiesta plating

Cut each quesadilla into three wedges and stack them slightly offset. Use salsa as a bright pool or side cup, not a blanket.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 3 oz chicken fajitas
  • 2 oz beef fajitas
  • 3 oz queso quesadilla, grated
  • ¼ small onion and ¼ bell pepper, diced
  • ½ garlic clove, chopped
  • Jalapeño to taste, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp. chopped cilantro
  • 1–2 tsp. oil
  • Salsa and lime

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and pepper in a little oil until lightly charred. Add garlic and jalapeño for 30 seconds.
  2. Chop the fajita meat and fold it into the vegetables with the queso and cilantro.
  3. Divide the filling between tortillas, fold, and cook in a lightly oiled skillet until golden and fully melted, about 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Rest one minute, cut into wedges, and serve with salsa and lime outside the quesadilla so it stays crisp.

Plate it

Add a cabbage-lime slaw if you bought cabbage; it gives the plate height and acidity without requiring crema.

Editorial source reviewed: Chicken Quesadillas — Bon Appétit. The sautéed pepper, onion, garlic, jalapeño, cilantro, and lightly oiled skillet technique are retained; the Meal Deal meats replace raw chicken.

Recipe 3 · Pantry bowl

Beef Fajita Quinoa Bowls

Use the existing tri-color quinoa rather than buying rice to duplicate the deal’s starch option.

Fiesta plating

Quinoa fills half the bowl. Arrange beef, beans, salsa, and avocado or cabbage in separate arcs; finish with queso at the center.

Ingredients

  • Cooked tri-color quinoa for 2
  • 5 oz beef fajitas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • ¼ red or white onion, finely chopped
  • Jalapeño to taste, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup cilantro, divided
  • 2 Tbsp. lime juice
  • 1 oz queso
  • Optional avocado, cabbage, or tortilla strips

Method

  1. Make a quick pico de gallo with tomato, onion, jalapeño, half the cilantro, lime juice, and salt.
  2. Season the quinoa with the remaining cilantro and a little lime.
  3. Crisp the cooked beef in a skillet with a pinch each of cumin and coriander and a splash of water; warm the beans separately.
  4. Build bowls with quinoa, beef, beans, and separate arcs of pico, avocado or cabbage. Finish with queso.

Editorial source reviewed: Easy Taco Bowls — Bon Appétit. The fresh pico and composed-bowl structure are retained; quinoa replaces rice and cooked fajita beef replaces ground beef.

Recipe 4 · Soup night

Chicken Fajita Fideo

Mi Tienda estrellas or letras pasta and stocked broth turn a small meat portion into a real soup.

Fiesta plating

Use a deep bowl. Float diced avocado and crumbled queso at the center; cilantro and lime go on at the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz chicken fajitas, chopped
  • 1 cup small pasta
  • 2 plum tomatoes, chopped
  • ¼ white onion, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp. oil
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • Queso, avocado, cilantro, and lime for serving

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, onion, and garlic until smooth; strain if you want a silkier broth.
  2. Heat the oil and toast the dry pasta, stirring often, until light golden, 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato purée and cook 1 minute. Add broth, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer just until the pasta is tender.
  4. Add the cooked chicken only for the final minute so it does not dry out. Season and serve with queso, avocado, cilantro, and lime.

Editorial source reviewed: Sopa de Fideo — Bon Appétit. The toasted-noodle and fresh tomato–onion–garlic base are retained; cooked fajita chicken is added only at the end.

Recipe 5 · Masa night

Fajita Masa Cakes With Beans And Queso

Mi Tienda masa makes the week feel less like reheating and more like a second cuisine.

Fiesta plating

Serve three small cakes per plate in a triangle. Keep toppings centered so the crisp brown edges remain visible.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups masa or masa harina
  • Warm water and salt as directed for the specific masa product
  • Oil for hands and skillet
  • 4 oz mixed fajita meat
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 oz queso
  • Salsa, cilantro, onion, and optional avocado

Method

  1. Mix or knead the masa until moist and tacky but not sticky; rest, covered, for 10 minutes so it hydrates evenly.
  2. Form six disks about ⅓ inch thick. If the edges crack, knead in water a teaspoon at a time.
  3. Lightly oil a skillet. Cook the disks covered until the first side is browned, then uncover, flip, and cook until the second side is dry and the cakes sound faintly hollow when tapped.
  4. Top rather than split: spread with hot beans, then add warmed meat, queso, salsa, cilantro, onion, and avocado.

Editorial source reviewed: Classic Arepas — Bon Appétit. Its hydration test, resting period, disk thickness, and covered-to-uncovered griddle method guide these cakes; follow the Mi Tienda package for water because prepared masa, masa harina, and masarepa are not interchangeable.

Recipe 6 · Crunch night

Baked Fajita Tostadas

The free tortillas become their own chips, avoiding another bagged purchase.

Fiesta plating

Use two tostadas per plate with a lime wedge between them. Contrast crisp tan tortillas with green cilantro and red salsa.

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 4 oz chicken or beef fajitas
  • ½ cup refried beans
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • ¼ small onion, finely chopped
  • Jalapeño and cilantro to taste
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Pinches of cumin, coriander, and smoked paprika
  • 1½ oz queso
  • Optional avocado or cabbage

Method

  1. Make pico de gallo with tomato, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  2. Brush tortillas lightly with oil and bake at 400°F, flipping once, until crisp.
  3. Chop and reheat the meat with cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, and a spoonful of water. Warm the beans separately.
  4. Layer each crisp tortilla with beans, meat, pico, queso, and avocado or cabbage. Serve immediately.

Editorial source reviewed: Spiced Beef and Refried Bean Tostadas — Bon Appétit. The spicing, refried-bean layer, fresh pico, and avocado finish are retained; cooked fajita meat and oven-crisped Meal Deal tortillas replace ground beef and packaged tostadas.

Recipe 7 · Brunch save

Fajita Chilaquiles Skillet

Use tortillas that are drying out; eggs are optional, not required for the base meal.

Fiesta plating

Serve directly from the skillet or in shallow bowls. Keep queso and onion on top so the softened tortillas still show.

Ingredients

  • 4 tortillas, cut into wedges
  • 4 oz mixed fajita meat
  • 1½ cups red salsa, divided
  • 1 oz queso
  • Optional 2–4 fried eggs
  • Optional avocado and crema
  • Cilantro and lime wedges

Method

  1. Lightly oil and bake or pan-fry the tortilla wedges until sturdy and crisp; season while hot.
  2. Warm the cooked meat with ¼ cup salsa in a small pan.
  3. Bring the remaining salsa to a simmer in a wide skillet. Add the crisp wedges and toss to coat.
  4. Cover, remove from heat, and rest 1 minute for mostly crisp chilaquiles or up to 3 minutes for softer ones.
  5. Plate immediately with the hot meat, queso, optional eggs, avocado or crema, cilantro, and lime. Serve beans alongside rather than stirring them into the chips.

Editorial source reviewed: Chilaquiles Rojos — Bon Appétit. The separate meat warming and timed salsa soak are retained; baked or shallow-fried Meal Deal tortillas replace deep-frying.

Recipe 8 · Clean-out

End-of-Deal Fajita Tortilla Soup

The intentionally flexible final meal catches whatever remains without disguising it as scraps.

Fiesta plating

Use the largest bowls. Put queso beneath the hot soup so it softens; add tortilla strips and herbs only at serving.

Ingredients

  • All remaining fajita meat
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • ⅓ white onion
  • 4 cups chicken or beef broth
  • Remaining tortillas, cut into thin strips
  • 1 pasilla chile, sliced into rings, optional
  • Epazote, cilantro, or both
  • Remaining queso, avocado, crema, and lime

Method

  1. Dry-roast the tomatoes, unpeeled garlic, and onion in a skillet until blistered. Peel the garlic and tomatoes, then blend the vegetables smooth with a little broth.
  2. Cook the purée in a spoonful of oil until darker and reduced, about 5–8 minutes. Add the remaining broth and simmer 10 minutes; add epazote if using.
  3. Crisp the tortilla strips in a skillet with oil; quickly crisp the pasilla rings separately, watching closely.
  4. Add the chopped cooked meat to the soup only long enough to heat. Season to taste.
  5. Put queso beneath the hot soup; finish with tortilla strips, pasilla, avocado, crema, herbs, and lime. Serve leftover beans on the side rather than muddying the tomato broth.

Editorial source reviewed: Tortilla Soup — Bon Appétit. The roasted tomato base, reduced purée, crisp tortilla strips, and pasilla garnish are retained; leftover cooked fajita meat is added only at the end.
