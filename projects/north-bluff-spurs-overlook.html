Hiking field guide · Government Canyon

North Bluff Spurs Overlook

A rocky backcountry climb to the edge of a limestone bluff and a broad view across Government Canyon.

Distance3.4 miles round trip

Time1.5–2 hours
Trail useHiking only; no pets

Why this hike matters

Climb onto the Edwards Plateau.

Government Canyon protects more than 13,000 acres over the Edwards Aquifer Recharge Zone. The route moves from an easy backcountry approach into a rocky climb, ending at a limestone edge above the canyonlands.

Read the rock

Notice exposed limestone, thin soils, fractures, and the way vegetation changes between shaded drainage and dry bluff.

Watch the transition

The official route begins gently, then climbs Sendero Balcones toward Bluff Spurs and the plateau rim.

Pause at the edge

The North Bluff Spurs Overlook sits at 29.5571, -98.7608. Stay back from exposed edges and keep to the designated trail.

Route notes

A moderate hike with a rugged finish.

Start

Begin at the Backcountry Trailhead and follow a short section of the Joe Johnston Route.

Climb

Take Recharge Trail to Sendero Balcones, then continue uphill to Bluff Spurs Trail.

Overlook

Follow Bluff Spurs to the North Overlook, then return on Bluff Spurs to Recharge Trail.

Conditions

Expect uneven, rocky terrain, exposure, and greater difficulty during South Texas heat.

Plan a responsible visit

  • Reserve a day pass before traveling; the park often reaches capacity.
  • Check current trail closures and weather before leaving home.
  • Bring substantial water, sun protection, snacks, and reliable footwear.
  • Leave pets at home; they are not permitted in the backcountry.
  • Hike Bluff Spurs rather than bike it, and remain on designated trails.
