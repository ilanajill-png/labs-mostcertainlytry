Ritual

A bright fruit-and-clay reset for receiving more without turning self-care into a second job. This is the house ceremony for freshness, enoughness, and practical plenty.

Prayer

Opening Prayer

May I receive what is already trying to feed me. May enough feel real in my body, not theoretical in my notes. Let this house remember abundance as stocked towels, clean water, warm light, and one simple next step.

Equipment Note

Skip the sauna blanket for this one unless doing a pre-scrub warmup. Oils and clay stay out of the blanket.

Human prep

Objects To Set Out

  • Mira Marfa Botnia Luminous Body Scrub - use as the main scrub.
  • Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Hydrating Shower Gel - use as the cleansing step.
  • Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Cactus Body Creme - use as the closing body cream.
  • Mira Marfa Orange and Amber Botanical Incense - light before the shower if using incense.
  • La Sandia card
  • citrus or lime sugar scrub
  • green clay or mineral mud
  • bright body wash
  • jojoba oil
  • body butter
  • hibiscus-lime water
  • dark towel
  • Comfier CF-2113 chair optional for closing massage

Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Place the La Sandia card on the tray, pour hibiscus-lime water, and light Mira Marfa Orange and Amber Botanical Incense if using incense.
  2. Start the playlist and set a warm daylight scene.
  3. Dry brush or towel-polish arms and legs.
  4. Shower with Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Hydrating Shower Gel.
  5. Use Mira Marfa Botnia Luminous Body Scrub from feet upward with gentle pressure, then rinse.
  6. Apply green clay or mineral mud to arms, legs, shoulders, or feet and rest for 8 to 10 minutes. Keep product away from the massage chair.
  7. Rinse fully, towel dry, apply jojoba oil if desired, then close with Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Cactus Body Creme.
  8. Sit in the massage chair for a gentle 10 minute closing cycle only when skin and hands are clean enough for upholstery.
  9. Name one practical abundance before leaving the room.
