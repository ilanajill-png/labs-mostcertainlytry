Ritual

A steamy coffee, rose, and water-return ceremony. This is the most theatrical home spa ritual and the one most likely to make the bathroom feel like a little hotel hammam.

Prayer

Opening Prayer

Waters below, waters within, carry off what is ready to loosen. Let beauty be warm, practical, and rinsable. Let me return to myself clean enough to begin again.

Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Place the card outside the splash zone and start the hammam playlist.
  2. Run a warm shower until the room is comfortable, not stifling.
  3. Apply coffee sugar scrub to body, avoiding face and delicate skin.
  4. Rinse thoroughly so grounds do not follow you like evidence.
  5. Apply rose clay to arms, legs, shoulders, or feet for 7 to 10 minutes.
  6. Rinse fully and mist with rose or orange blossom.
  7. Apply warm floral oil, then body butter on dry spots.
  8. Use the massage chair only after hands and body are clean enough for upholstery.
  9. Rest in robe with mint tea.
