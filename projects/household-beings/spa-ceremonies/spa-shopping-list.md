# Las Jaras Spa Ceremonies Shopping List

This is the consolidated purchase list for all nine Las Jaras Loteria spa ceremonies. It assumes the Comfier CF-2113 massage chair and LifePro far infrared sauna blanket are already available at home.

## Buy First: Core Kit For All Ceremonies

- Garshana gloves or a soft dry brush.
- Gentle citrus or lime sugar scrub.
- Fine sea salt scrub.
- Unscented jojoba body oil.
- Vata or warming body oil.
- Rich body butter.
- Cloud-like body cream.
- Mineral mud or thermal mud.
- Rose clay or floral/botanical body mask.
- Eucalyptus or lavender shower mist.
- Scalp massager or scalp brush.
- Scalp scrub or gentle scalp treatment.
- Dark towel set for clay, coffee, turmeric, and oil ceremonies.
- Pale towel set for clean/floral ceremonies.
- Hair towel or wrap.
- Washcloth stack.
- Waterproof timer.
- Cobalt tray or shallow ritual tray.
- Loteria card stand.
- Journal and pen.
- Reusable water cup or carafe.

## Pantry And Beverage Supplies

- Hibiscus tea.
- Mint tea.
- Chamomile tea.
- Tulsi tea.
- Green tea.

## Optional Upgrades

- Cobalt robe hook or towel ladder.
- Dedicated spa caddy.
- Glass spray bottle for hydrosol or shower mist.
- Essential oil diffuser, if scents are tolerated.
- Bluetooth speaker or dedicated spa speaker.
- Small waterproof speaker backup.
- Pretty tea tray.

- Printed one-page menu for the bathroom or cabinet door.

## Do Not Buy Until Tested

- Strong ginger essential oil.
- Turmeric-heavy body products.
- Aggressive exfoliating mitts.
- Highly fragranced body oils.
- Any body wrap products that require tight wrapping, long heat exposure, or complicated cleanup.

## Already Available Or Not A Purchase Priority

- Comfier CF-2113 massage chair.
- LifePro far infrared sauna blanket.
- Existing towels, if enough can be dedicated to clay/oil work.
- Existing robe, pajamas, and tea mugs.

## Reorder Triggers

- Scrubs: reorder when one-third full.
- Oils: reorder when one-quarter full.
- Body cream/body butter: reorder when one-third full.
- Clay/mud masks: reorder after 3 remaining full-body or 6 partial-body uses.
- Teas: reorder when fewer than 6 bags/servings remain.
- Towels/washcloths: replace when oil smell or clay staining survives a normal wash.
