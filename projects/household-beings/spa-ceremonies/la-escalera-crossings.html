Ritual

A threshold ceremony for travel returns, new projects, hard conversations, or any moment where the body needs help crossing from one chapter to the next.

Prayer

Opening Prayer

May I know the step I am actually on. May I stop asking the whole staircase to appear before I move. Bless the crossing, bless the pause, bless the small honest next thing.

Equipment Note

Best used before oil as the warm threshold. Hydrate, avoid overheating, and keep creams/oils out of the blanket.

Human prep

Objects To Set Out

  • Mira Marfa Relaxing Bath Salts - use for a bath or foot soak version.
  • Mira Marfa No. 5 Lavandin + Chamomile Candle - use for the threshold calm scene.
  • Mira Marfa Basil Yarrow Mist - use after showering as the reset mist.
  • Mira Marfa Cactus Hand Cream - Flor de Madera - use before journaling.
  • La Escalera card
  • garshana gloves or dry brush
  • unscented jojoba or Vata body oil
  • robe or warm towel
  • journal and pen
  • tulsi or chamomile tea
  • LifePro sauna blanket optional before oil
  • Comfier CF-2113 chair optional after shower

Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Place the card where it can be seen and write one sentence about what is ending.
  2. Start the playlist and set the threshold calm light scene.
  3. Use garshana gloves for 3 to 5 minutes with gentle strokes.
  4. Optional: use the sauna blanket before oil, on a low comfortable setting, with water nearby and according to the manual.
  5. Shower warmly and dry off.
  6. Apply body oil slowly, limb by limb.
  7. Use the massage chair for a gentle cycle or sit under a weighted blanket.
  8. Write one before sentence and one after sentence.
  9. Put one object away or choose one next step to close the crossing.
