Equipment Note

Best fit for this ceremony if used before shower and before any oils. Prefer candle-free if drowsy.

Human prep

Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Set night path lighting, put pajamas where they are easy to reach, and place the La Luna card on the bedside table.
  2. Start the bedtime playlist. If using Mira Marfa No. 5 Lavandin + Chamomile Candle, light it only while alert and switch to flameless before you get sleepy.
  3. Optional: use the sauna blanket before showering, at a comfortable setting, with water nearby. Stop if too hot or sleepy.
  4. If this becomes a bath or foot soak, use Mira Marfa Relaxing Bath Salts before the shower step.
  5. Mist shower with eucalyptus or lavender, then use fine salt scrub on feet, hands, and shoulders.
  6. Rinse and use scalp scrub or scalp brush gently.
  7. Mist with Mira Marfa Basil Yarrow Mist if it feels restful.
  8. Apply light body oil if desired, then Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Cactus Body Creme.
  9. Put on pajamas, apply Mira Marfa Cactus Hand Cream, lower lights, and write one line: Tomorrow can hold ___.
