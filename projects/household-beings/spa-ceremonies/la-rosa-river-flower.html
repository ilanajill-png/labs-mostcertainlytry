Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Place the card with a flower, leaf, or beautiful object.
  2. Start the calming spa playlist and set rose-gold light.
  3. Dry brush lightly.
  4. Take a warm shower.
  5. Apply botanical mask to feet, calves, arms, or shoulders.
  6. Rinse and dry.
  7. Apply rose or petal body butter.
  8. Choose either foot massage or scalp massage.
  9. Use the massage chair if it feels nurturing, then place the flower or object somewhere visible.
