Guardrails

House Rules Before Heat, Scent, Or Scrub

Nanny Hearth Says

Patch test new products, hydrate, skip strong heat on fragile days, and stop if skin, breath, dizziness, or mood says no.

Officer Ember Says

Keep cords clear, floors dry, candles away from fabric, and sauna blanket use boringly safe. Read the product manuals. Glamour is not a waiver.
