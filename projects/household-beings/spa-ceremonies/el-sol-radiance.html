Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Set the card near the mirror and choose the robe or pajamas before starting.
  2. Start the positive instrumental playlist and set flattering daylight.
  3. Use gentle scrub in the shower.
  4. Rinse and apply mineral mud to arms, legs, or shoulders for 8 minutes.
  5. Rinse thoroughly and dry.
  6. Use scalp massager for 3 minutes.
  7. Apply antioxidant body cream.
  8. Use the massage chair for a confident reset cycle.
  9. Put on the chosen robe or pajamas and look in the mirror without picking yourself apart.
