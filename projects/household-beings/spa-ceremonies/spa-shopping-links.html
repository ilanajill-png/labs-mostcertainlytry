Researched June 17, 2026. Prices, stock, and sale status can change. This list picks reliable retailers and practical product matches for the nine Las Jaras Loteria spa ceremonies.

Purchase Strategy

  • Best all-around retailers: Target for towels, scrubs, bath basics, tea, and pickup convenience; Ulta/Sephora for beauty products; Miraval/ZENTS/Banyan for spa-specific ritual products; Amazon for tools and setup objects.
  • Do not buy everything twice: several products cover multiple ceremonies.
  • Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, creams, scrubs, or clay. Do not put oily or muddy products inside the blanket.
  • Patch test body products before using them in a full ritual.

Mira Marfa Fits

Mira Marfa works best for boutique ceremony upgrades: bath salts, body scrub, shower gel, body cream, mists, incense, candles, and small face/hand finishing products. I would not use Mira Marfa for the practical basics like towels, non-slip mats, card stands, or the main clay/mud kit.
