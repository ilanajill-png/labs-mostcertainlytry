Ritual

Prayer

Opening Prayer

Root me without trapping me. Let the living things ask nothing extra. May I borrow steadiness from shade, wood, water, and the ground under this house.

Human prep

Objects To Set Out

  • Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Hydrating Shower Gel - use as the desert botanical wash.
  • Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Cactus Body Creme - use as body or foot cream.
  • Mira Marfa Grass Blend Mineral Mist - use as the outdoor green finishing mist.
  • Mira Marfa No. 3 Moss + Amber Candle - use for the wooded indoor version.
  • El Arbol card
  • almond polish or nut-free gentle scrub
  • citrus body oil
  • foot cream
  • cucumber water or herbal tea
  • porch-safe robe or sandals
  • Comfier CF-2113 chair optional

Shared Heat Rule

Use the LifePro sauna blanket before oils, clays, scrubs, or creams. Follow the product manual, hydrate, keep the floor safe, and stop if heat feels wrong. Do not climb into a blanket or massage chair covered in product unless you are prepared to become the cautionary tale.

Self-led flow

How To Perform The Ritual

  1. Check weather and porch conditions before committing to the outdoor part.
  2. Place the El Arbol card beside a leaf, stone, or wooden object and light Mira Marfa No. 3 Moss + Amber Candle if using flame.
  3. Start the nature playlist and set golden-hour lighting.
  4. Step outside or open a window for 2 minutes if weather is kind.
  5. Shower with Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Hydrating Shower Gel, then use almond or nut-free polish on hands, feet, elbows, knees, and shoulders.
  6. Rinse, dry, and apply citrus oil if desired.
  7. Mist with Mira Marfa Grass Blend Mineral Mist, then apply Mira Marfa Dulce de Cuerpo Cactus Body Creme.
  8. Massage foot cream into feet.
  9. Sit outside briefly or use the massage chair for a low restorative cycle after products have absorbed.
  10. Name one living thing that needs nothing from you right now.
