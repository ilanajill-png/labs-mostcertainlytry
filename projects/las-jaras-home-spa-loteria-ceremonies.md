# Las Jaras Home Spa Loteria Ceremonies

This plan adapts the nine Miraval-inspired home spa ceremonies into Las Jaras rituals. Each ceremony is tied to a Loteria card, uses at-home products rather than professional bodywork, and gives every household being a clear job.

## Operating Model

**Home spa cabinet:** Soma Hearthline owns the room state, Guru Marigold owns the meaning, Nanny Hearth owns comfort and common-sense guardrails, and Sundries owns supplies.

**Default duration:** 45 to 75 minutes.

**Default location:** bathroom plus bedroom or quiet sitting room for the closing rest.

**Default safety rules:**

- Patch test new body products before using them in a ceremony.
- Dilute essential oils in carrier oil. Do not apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
- Skip strong heat, aggressive exfoliation, ginger oils, or full-body wrapping if skin is irritated, sunburned, freshly shaved, or medically fussy.
- Keep the floor dry enough to avoid slipping.
- Ceremonies are for comfort, beauty, and mood. They are not medical treatment.

## Standard Being Assignments

**Soma Hearthline**

- Set the light scene, temperature, fan, music/sound fallback, and quiet mode.
- Create a physical fallback for every scene: one lamp, one towel, one timer, one visible button or manual switch.
- Audit the ceremony afterward: did the house feel calmer, or did the setup become a project?

**Guru Marigold**

- Choose the intention, Loteria card, opening prompt, and closing reflection.
- Keep the ceremony grounded, not overwrought.
- Decide whether the ceremony is solo, guest-friendly, or recovery-only.

**Nanny Hearth**

- Put water or tea within reach.
- Check skin sensitivity, heat tolerance, allergies, fatigue, and post-ceremony rest needs.
- Stop anything that feels irritating, dizzy, too hot, too fragrant, or too much.

**Sundries**

- Maintain the spa inventory: scrubs, oils, masks, body butter, washcloths, bath soak, shower mist, gloves, candles, and cleanup supplies.
- Track low stock and preferred brands.
- Prepare shopping suggestions without checking out.

**Velvet Thimble**

- Style the tray, robe, towel stack, product placement, candle line, and color story.
- Keep the ceremony visually Las Jaras: cobalt, terracotta, marigold, sage, rose, carved wood, hotel-rich texture.

**Madame Spin**

- Prepare clean towels, robe, hair wrap, washcloths, and post-ceremony laundry guidance.
- Flag oils, clays, turmeric, coffee, and botanicals that may stain.

**Keeper Brisk**

- Pre-reset the bathroom, bedroom path, and surfaces.
- Handle the post-ceremony reset checklist so relaxation does not leave a weird little disaster behind.

**DJ Bluehour**

- Provide a playlist matched to the card: instrumental, quiet, no jarring ads or sudden volume jumps.
- Keep a no-phone backup, such as a downloaded playlist or simple speaker preset.

**Barista Luma**

- Pair each ceremony with tea, infused water, or a light beverage.
- Keep caffeine out of evening ceremonies unless buddy wants a tiny glamorous bad decision.

**Officer Ember**

- Check candles, cords, hot water, slippery floors, ventilation, and blocked exits.
- Kill any ceremony step that creates a real hazard.

**Archivist Vellum**

- Record what worked, what products were used, skin reactions, favorite scents, and future changes.
- Keep a private source list for product research and purchase notes.

**Concierge Paloma**

- Convert solo ceremonies into guest-safe versions: simpler products, allergy questions, house instructions, and a graceful ending.

## Ceremony 1: La Sandia Abundance

**Source inspiration:** Abundance ritual: fruit, clay, polish, nourishment.

**Loteria card:** La Sandia.

**Las Jaras name:** La Sandia Abundance.

**Use when:** The house needs a generous reset, the bathroom needs to feel like a fruit stand and a boutique hotel had a sensible child, or buddy wants a "fresh start but make it juicy" ceremony.

**Core products:**

- Citrus or lime sugar scrub.
- Green clay, spirulina clay, or mineral mud.
- Bright body wash.
- Jojoba oil.
- Rich body butter.
- Hibiscus, lime, or cucumber water.

**Sequence:**

1. Guru opens with the prompt: "Where am I ready to receive more without making it complicated?"
2. Soma sets warm daylight, a slightly cooler bathroom, and a soft morning playlist.
3. Dry brush or lightly towel-polish arms and legs.
4. Apply citrus sugar scrub from feet upward, using gentle pressure.
5. Rinse.
6. Apply green clay or mineral mud to arms, legs, shoulders, or feet. Skip face unless the product is made for face.
7. Rest for 8 to 10 minutes with water nearby.
8. Rinse, towel dry, apply jojoba oil, then body butter.
9. Guru closes by naming one practical abundance: stocked towels, paid bill, full fridge, answered message, watered plant.

**Being tasks:**

- Soma: "golden fruit" light scene, fan on low, no harsh overhead glare.
- Guru: opening prompt and closing gratitude.
- Nanny: water and skin-sensitivity check.
- Sundries: confirm scrub, clay, oil, and butter are stocked.
- Velvet: tray with citrus, cobalt dish, folded cloth.
- Madame Spin: dark towel if using green clay.
- Keeper: rinse tub/shower before clay dries.
- DJ: bright acoustic or soft Latin instrumental.
- Barista: hibiscus-lime water.
- Officer Ember: slip check after oil step.
- Archivist: record whether citrus or clay irritated skin.

## Ceremony 2: La Escalera Crossings

**Source inspiration:** Crossings ceremony: intention, garshana, wrap, weighted rest, transition.

**Loteria card:** La Escalera.

**Las Jaras name:** La Escalera Crossings.

**Use when:** A threshold is happening: after travel, before a hard conversation, on a new moon, at the start of a house project, or when buddy wants to stop carrying one room of the past into the next.

**Core products:**

- Garshana gloves or dry brush.
- Gentle body oil, preferably Vata or unscented jojoba.
- Warm towel or robe.
- Weighted blanket.
- Simple body cream.
- Chamomile, tulsi, or mint tea.

**Sequence:**

1. Guru places the La Escalera card where it can be seen and asks: "What step am I actually on?"
2. Soma sets a low, steady, non-theatrical light scene.
3. Dry brush or use garshana gloves for 3 to 5 minutes, always gentle.
4. Warm shower.
5. Apply body oil slowly, limb by limb.
6. Put on robe or warm towel and rest under weighted blanket for 12 minutes.
7. Sip tea.
8. Write one "before" sentence and one "after" sentence.
9. Close by putting one object away, sending one note, or choosing one next step.

**Being tasks:**

- Soma: "threshold calm" scene with low amber light and quiet doorbell mode.
- Guru: card reading and transition prompt.
- Nanny: check that weighted blanket is comfortable and not too hot.
- Sundries: keep garshana gloves, oil, and tea stocked.
- Velvet: place the card on a small tray with one stone or key.
- Madame Spin: warm robe/towel and protect linens from oil.
- Keeper: clear floor path from shower to rest spot.
- DJ: slow drone, ambient piano, or desert evening playlist.
- Barista: tulsi or chamomile tea.
- Officer Ember: trip-hazard check before low lighting.
- Archivist: log the threshold and chosen next step.

## Ceremony 3: El Nopal Ginger Healer

**Source inspiration:** Ginger Healer: warming oil, salt scrub, deep reset.

**Loteria card:** El Nopal.

**Las Jaras name:** El Nopal Ginger Healer.

**Use when:** The body feels prickly, stagnant, chilled, or overworked and needs warmth without drama.

**Core products:**

- Ginger-scented body oil or very lightly diluted ginger essential oil in jojoba.
- Sea salt scrub.
- Warm compress.
- Unscented body cream.
- Ginger tea or lemon water.

**Sequence:**

1. Guru asks: "What protection has helped me, and what can soften now?"
2. Soma warms the room slightly and sets a terracotta evening scene.
3. Use salt scrub on feet, calves, hands, and forearms. Avoid sensitive areas.
4. Rinse with warm water.
5. Apply ginger body oil only where tolerated, or use unscented oil if skin is reactive.
6. Place warm compress on shoulders or belly for 5 minutes.
7. Massage hands, feet, jaw, and scalp lightly.
8. Finish with unscented body cream.
9. Close with a glass of water.

**Being tasks:**

- Soma: warm room scene, fan off unless needed for ventilation.
- Guru: protection-to-softening reflection.
- Nanny: veto strong ginger on sensitive skin, pregnancy concerns, migraines, or irritation.
- Sundries: stock ginger tea, salt scrub, carrier oil, and compress cloth.
- Velvet: prickly-pear, terracotta, and linen styling.
- Madame Spin: use oil-safe towel.
- Keeper: scrub residue removal.
- DJ: low percussion or grounding instrumental.
- Barista: ginger tea, weak and not punishing.
- Officer Ember: hot compress temperature check.
- Archivist: record whether ginger is loved or banished.

## Ceremony 4: La Sirena Hammam

**Source inspiration:** Hammam: coffee-sugar exfoliation, rose/jasmine clay, orange blossom mist, warm oil.

**Loteria card:** La Sirena.

**Las Jaras name:** La Sirena Hammam.

**Use when:** Buddy wants the most theatrical, steamy, rose-and-coffee version of the home spa.

**Core products:**

- Coffee sugar scrub.
- Rose clay or jasmine clay mask.
- Orange blossom or rose hydrosol.
- Cardamom, amber, or warm floral body oil.
- Fig, citrus, or rose body butter.
- Mint tea.

**Sequence:**

1. Guru asks: "What part of me wants to return to the water?"
2. Soma creates steam-friendly light: low glow, no blue-white brightness.
3. Shower until the room is warm, not suffocating.
4. Apply coffee sugar scrub to body, avoiding face and delicate skin.
5. Rinse thoroughly.
6. Apply rose clay to arms, legs, shoulders, or feet for 7 to 10 minutes.
7. Mist with orange blossom or rose after rinsing.
8. Apply warm floral oil, then body butter on dry spots.
9. Rest wrapped in robe with mint tea.

**Being tasks:**

- Soma: steam-room glow, ventilation ready, mirror light low.
- Guru: water-return prompt and closing breath.
- Nanny: avoid overheating and over-scrubbing.
- Sundries: coffee scrub, rose clay, hydrosol, oil, butter.
- Velvet: brass/cobalt tray, rose towel, small bowl for clay.
- Madame Spin: coffee stain precautions.
- Keeper: remove coffee grounds from shower immediately.
- DJ: hammam-adjacent instrumental, no spa-flute nonsense unless requested.
- Barista: mint tea.
- Officer Ember: candle and slippery-floor check.
- Archivist: note whether coffee grounds are worth the cleanup.

## Ceremony 5: El Corazon Kokoro

**Source inspiration:** Kokoro ritual: ginger grass, bamboo, rice polish, shiatsu-inspired calm, wild lime oil.

**Loteria card:** El Corazon.

**Las Jaras name:** El Corazon Kokoro.

**Use when:** The ceremony should feel precise, heartful, and quiet rather than lush.

**Core products:**

- Rice bran polish or gentle rice/bamboo body scrub.
- Lime or yuzu body oil.
- Light floral or plum body cream.
- Scalp massager.
- Green tea.

**Sequence:**

1. Guru asks: "What would care look like if it were exact, not excessive?"
2. Soma sets clean, balanced light with a clear path and minimal clutter.
3. Wash hands first as a deliberate start.
4. Use rice/bamboo polish gently on arms, shoulders, chest, and legs.
5. Rinse.
6. Apply lime/yuzu body oil in slow symmetrical passes.
7. Use thumb pressure on palms and soles, very light and never painful.
8. Finish with floral cream.
9. Sit for 5 minutes in silence with green tea.

**Being tasks:**

- Soma: minimal light scene, quiet fan, no app fiddling mid-ceremony.
- Guru: precision-care prompt.
- Nanny: keep pressure gentle and non-therapeutic.
- Sundries: rice polish, lime oil, cream, tea.
- Velvet: uncluttered tray, one flower, folded white cloth.
- Madame Spin: pale towel only if oils are non-staining.
- Keeper: clear counter fully before starting.
- DJ: barely-there koto, piano, or silence.
- Barista: green tea brewed correctly, not bitter.
- Officer Ember: ensure floor is dry before foot pressure.
- Archivist: record the exact sequence if this becomes a favorite.

## Guest Version

Concierge Paloma may convert any ceremony into a guest version by simplifying it to three steps:

1. Scented shower or foot soak.
2. Gentle scrub or body cream.
3. Tea, robe, and quiet room reset.

Guest versions should avoid strong essential oils, aggressive exfoliation, body wraps, or products with common allergens unless the guest has opted in.
