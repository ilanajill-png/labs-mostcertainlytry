Simplified fall garden plan

A field-readable plan based on the timber lanes visible in IMG_2838. View is from the west entrance, matching the photograph: north is left, south is right, and the herb spiral is toward the east/far end. The drawing is schematic; existing timber edges control the field layout.

Direct-sow depths

  • Carrots: ¼–½ in; thin to 2 in.
  • Scallions: ¼–½ in; thin to 1 in.
  • Broccoli: ½ in; 2–3 seeds per station.
  • Cauliflower: ½ in; 2–3 seeds per station.

Succession rule

Broccoli and cauliflower seedlings move into gaps left after radish, turnip, kohlrabi, pak choi, or lettuce harvest. Mature cole crops still require roughly 18–24 inches.

Watering-can rule

Keep seed surfaces evenly damp with a gentle rose. Water established transplants deeply at their root zones only after the two-inch soil check shows drying.

Field check

If a timber lane is narrower than 4 feet, use one staggered row rather than two full rows. The photograph establishes the layout, but not survey-grade lane widths.
