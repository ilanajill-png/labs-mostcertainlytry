Timing

Expect an all-day run: roughly 4 to 5 hours of driving before parking, checking seed stock, photos, breaks, and sorting. A two-day split is easier: north/west stops first, then central/south/east stops. If Apple Maps drops intermediate stops, add them manually from the stop order.
