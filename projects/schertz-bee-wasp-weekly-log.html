    Transparent boundary

    Know what the checklist can—and cannot—say.

    The local species explorer uses iNaturalist research-grade records in a fixed regional bounding box. It excludes ants, separates bees by taxonomic ancestry, and treats the remaining narrow-waisted Hymenoptera as wasps. Full query details, limitations, privacy behavior, and source links are recorded on the methods page.
