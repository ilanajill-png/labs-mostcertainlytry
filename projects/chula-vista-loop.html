Hiking field guide · Government Canyon

Chula Vista Loop

A longer backcountry circuit through rocky canyonlands to one of Government Canyon’s broadest scenic overlooks.

Why this hike matters

Take the long view of the canyonlands.

The loop links Far Reaches Trail and Sendero Balcones to Chula Vista, crossing the rugged Edwards Plateau landscape protected by Government Canyon State Natural Area.

Overlook country

Chula Vista opens a long view across limestone ridges, wooded drainages, and the natural area’s protected recharge landscape.

Changing terrain

Expect rocky tread, exposed stretches, and repeated transitions between dry ridge vegetation and denser canyon cover.

A linked route

This community-mapped loop uses Far Reaches Trail and Sendero Balcones and passes the North Bluff Spurs Overlook area.

Route notes

Moderate mileage, backcountry consequences.

Route type

A 5.8-mile loop assembled from named trails on TPWD’s official Government Canyon map.

Climbing

Gaia GPS reports 338 feet of elevation gain across public tracks for this route.

Navigation

Carry the current TPWD map and confirm every junction; do not rely on the field guide alone for navigation.

Heat

The open, rocky backcountry can make a moderate route feel much harder in hot weather.

Plan a responsible visit

  • Reserve a day pass before traveling; the park often reaches capacity.
  • Check current trail closures and weather immediately before departure.
  • Carry the official trail map, substantial water, sun protection, and snacks.
  • Wear dependable footwear for uneven limestone and loose rock.
  • Leave pets at home; they are not permitted in the backcountry.
