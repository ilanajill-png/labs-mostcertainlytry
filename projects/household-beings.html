Operating rule

Titles clarify care: who listens, who lowers friction, who protects privacy, who drafts the next move, and who is allowed to close the loop.

How they interact

Household Flow

1. Listen

Serafina, Sheriff, Sundries, Mailman, Webmaster, and Soma receive reports, forms, public notes, comfort cues, or follow-up needs.

2. Soothe

Soma checks nervous-system impact, Sheriff ranks risk, Sundries checks inventory, Tock checks timing, and Nanny or Guru flags human-care context.

3. Assign

The right specialist takes jurisdiction: Living System, Kitchen, Care, Experience, Public Face, Housekeeping, or Infrastructure.

4. Act Quietly

Each being drafts, researches, schedules, cooks, cleans, posts, files, or prepares a human-reviewed next step with no visible fuss.

5. Close Loop

Mailman follows up, Webmaster records public-safe build notes, Soma updates the comfort checklist, and Sheriff closes cases only when the human says so.

Research notes protocol

Every being keeps watch for useful ideas.

Each position researches new purchases, articles, references, tools, products, and inspiration inside its jurisdiction. When something is interesting, the being makes a short note explaining why it matters, what decision it may affect, and where it should live.

Private notes are the source of truth

Use Las Jaras working notes for links, prices, cautions, decisions, and next actions. This is where purchase-relevant research belongs before anything becomes public.

Pinterest is for visual memory

Use Pinterest for rooms, gardens, materials, lighting mood, textiles, table settings, bath rituals, xeriscape, and product lookalikes. It is a mood board, not the operating record.

Instagram is for finished story

Use Instagram only after an idea becomes public-safe and beautiful: before/after moments, rituals, product trials, and house lore. It should not be the research archive.
