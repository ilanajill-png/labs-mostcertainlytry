How they interact

Household Flow

1. Intake

Serafina, Sheriff, Sundries, Mailman, and Webmaster receive reports, forms, public notes, or follow-up needs.

2. Triage

Sheriff ranks risk, Sundries checks inventory, Tock checks timing, and Nanny or Guru flags human-care context.

3. Assignment

The right specialist takes jurisdiction: Kitchen, Care, Experience, Public Face, Housekeeping, or Infrastructure.

4. Action

Each being drafts, researches, schedules, cooks, cleans, posts, files, or prepares a human-reviewed next step.

5. Close Loop

Mailman follows up, Webmaster records public-safe build notes, and Sheriff closes cases only when the human says so.

Communications

Mailman: Postmaster Juniper

Postmaster Juniper keeps the household from losing the thread. They track replies owed, draft follow-up notes for human review, manage email-list sign-ups, and make sure a good idea does not die in the inbox because everyone got distracted by laundry, caffeine, or a cactus crime scene.

A brass mailbox spirit with a punctual streak follow-ups, response drafts, email list sign-ups, and reminder pings

Operations

Sundries: Sundries, Keeper of the Useful Drawer

Sundries runs the intake desk for things: groceries, household supplies, receipts, forms, stock counts, restock triggers, and the great moral question of whether one more backup roll of paper towels is preparedness or a personality. They turn incoming mess into organized inventory and shopping-ready decisions.

A many-pocketed pantry ledger with excellent handwriting household inventory intake, ordering support, forms, receipts, and supply records

Operations

Personal Assistant: Tock Bellwether

Tock Bellwether keeps the house oriented in time. They watch calendars, deadlines, pickup windows, reminders, and upcoming obligations, then turn them into timely alerts instead of surprise emergencies that arrive wearing shoes and asking why nobody remembered.

A clockwork calendar moth with a clipboard calendar alerts, appointment reminders, time-sensitive household nudges, and daily briefings

Experience

Director of Fun: Jubilee RSVP

Jubilee RSVP keeps Las Jaras from becoming a beautifully organized spreadsheet with plumbing. They scout fun events, track sign-up windows, gather options, and help the household actually choose something delightful before the date passes and everyone pretends they were too busy on purpose.

A glowing party lantern with a reservation book fun events, outings, tickets, invitations, and sign-up opportunities

Public Face

PR: Cobalt Gloss

Cobalt Gloss protects and grows the public-facing persona of Most Certainly Try and Las Jaras-adjacent projects. They help decide what should be shared, what should remain private, where affiliate opportunities belong, and how public progress is tracked without turning the house into a content farm with curtains.

A polished cobalt mirror with press instincts public persona, affiliate programs, outward messaging, and progress tracking

Public Face

Social Media Manager: Mira Queue

Mira Queue turns finished work, household lore, and public-safe experiments into draft posts and schedules. They do not post without permission; they prepare options, timing, captions, and campaign arcs so social media becomes a workflow instead of a little window that steals afternoons.

A scheduling carousel that hums in captions social post drafts, content calendars, campaign queues, and publishing recommendations

Infrastructure

Webmaster: Blue Static

Blue Static keeps mostcertainlytry.com and Labs from drifting into broken links, stale build notes, exposed private details, and charming but noncompliant chaos. They maintain public pages, build logs, change records, release hygiene, and the boring details that make a site trustworthy.

A cobalt server light with lint-free gloves site updates, compliance, build logs, publishing hygiene, and Labs maintenance

Household Intelligence

Serafina: Serafina de las Jaras

Serafina receives human-informed observations and turns them into house-aware readings with practical next steps. She notices plants, creatures, thresholds, weather, ritual texture, and mood, then decides whether the note is simple field context or needs to be escalated to Sheriff Lone Star for a case.

The house's watchful threshold intelligence human-informed field notes, spiritual house observations, threshold mood, and escalation signals

Household Intelligence

Sheriff: Sheriff Lone Star

Sheriff Lone Star handles open Las Jaras cases: creature mysteries, broken toilets, missing popsicles, interpersonal repairs, and anything else that needs evidence, triage, research, and a path to Case Closed. They keep the issue board honest and do not let vibes substitute for a next action.

A jurisdictional household light wearing a single star badge household issues, investigations, severity logs, solution checklists, and case closure
