Living Room

Main social room, warm media room.

The house's main social room: movie nights, guest arrival, conversation, TV/audio, and warm character lighting. The seating anchor is a Room & Board Reese curved sectional, so lighting should wrap the couch softly instead of blasting the room from one direction.

El SolReese curved sectional3 fixture candidatesSamsung TVMedia accent optionalAudit before order

Loteria Card

El Sol makes this the warm center of the house: gathering, welcome, dining, TV, and conversation.

Furniture Anchor

  • Couch: Room & Board Reese 115x115" three-piece curved sectional.
  • Color: Orla Ivory from the linked Room & Board reference.
  • Dimensions: overall 115"w x 115"d x 32"h; inside seating 87"w x 87"d.
  • Seat: 19"h x 22"d; arm height 24"h; frame depth 34"; leg height 8"; legs removable.
  • Lighting impact: place the main floor-lamp test so it grazes the sectional from a side or back-corner angle.
Product Reference

Dining Cluster

  • Table: present; dimensions and finish pending.
  • Chairs: 4 Anthropologie Elowen Dining Chairs.
  • Material: plush cotton velvet upholstery with mixed wildlife and botanical print.
  • Construction: foam/fiber cushion, kiln-dried hardwood frame, webbed seat, hammered iron legs, back brass handle.
  • Chair dimensions: 24"w x 24.25"d x 34.25"h; seat 19.75"h x 16.75"d.
  • Care: professionally clean only.
  • Lighting impact: table lighting should reveal velvet texture and pattern while keeping the surface usable.
Chair Reference

Lighting Candidates

  • Floor lamp: primary warm ambient layer and best first test if it has a normal E26/A19 socket.
  • Flamingo lamp: character accent only; use low coral, rose, amber, or off.
  • Table chandelier/light: hold until the fixture type is clear. It may be E12, enclosed, integrated, or decorative.

Proposed Hue Scenes

  • Guest Arrival: warm cream, candle amber, low coral accent.
  • Movie Night: very dim amber with optional future TV glow.
  • Conversation: warm white plus table amber and soft flamingo accent.
  • Dining / Lingering Dinner: warm amber and cream on the table surface, with soft coral/rose only as accent.
  • Cleaning / Reset: bright warm white, no saturated color.

TV / Media

Samsung Smart TV is present, but model and integration are pending. A Hue Play gradient lightstrip, light bar, or tube could be a later movie-scene upgrade after TV size, power, outlet, cable routing, and the TV/sectional relationship are confirmed.

Audit Before Ordering

  • Socket/base closeups for all three lights.
  • Dining table dimensions, chair clearance, and table-light position.
  • Shade/cavity clearance and max-wattage labels.
  • Switch, dimmer, pull-chain, or inline control behavior.
  • Plug/cord routing and trip/heat risks.

Playlist Status

Decision So Far

Do not buy a Living Room-specific set yet. If the pilot bulbs work in standard lamps, test one Hue color A19/E26 in the floor lamp first, then decide what the flamingo and table light actually need.
