Assumption status

  • Parcel dimensions are planning-scale from 10,275 sqft and the 75 ft x 137 ft working estimate.
  • Parcel rotation is shown as an approximate true-north angle until a GIS bearing is captured.
  • Structures are area-informed placeholders, not a surveyed footprint.
  • Sun zones use South Texas exposure logic: west and south are the heat-management problem.

Immediate use

  • Put heat-tolerant beds in the full-sun band.
  • Use east-edge morning light for tender herbs and containers.
  • Keep west-edge planting conservative until shade and irrigation are known.
  • Use the deck/storage cluster as the first staging zone for garden workflow tools.

Watering assumptions

  • Summer plan should assume high evapotranspiration and hard afternoon heat.
  • Raised beds get drip or soaker lines; containers need faster visual checks.
  • Watering should happen early morning unless weather makes that wasteful.
  • Rain skip rule belongs in the Morning House Brief once weather automation is active.

Measurement checklist

  • Confirm front, rear, and side setbacks.
  • Measure actual house-to-fence distances.
  • Place detached storage, portable storage, deck, hose bibs, spigots, and outlets.
  • Capture shade photos at morning, noon, and late afternoon.
