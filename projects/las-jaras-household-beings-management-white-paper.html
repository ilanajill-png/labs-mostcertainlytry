Las Jaras operating whitepaper

Why The House Is Managed By Beings

Las Jaras has too many overlapping systems to manage as one generic checklist. The household-being model gives each area a name, jurisdiction, tone, and decision boundary so the house can be understood, explained, researched, and improved without losing the texture that makes it feel alive.

House operations Workflow design Human review Research memory

Executive Summary

The household beings are not decoration. They are a management interface. Each being owns a coherent slice of the house: food, safety, hospitality, styling, infrastructure, memory, research, money, comfort, and public presentation. This makes housework easier to assign, easier to review, and easier to explain to a person or an AI agent.

The Problem

A House Is Not One System

Las Jaras contains food inventory, shopping, guest readiness, weather, garden care, safety, smart-home scenes, room moods, design history, receipts, public storytelling, and maintenance. One flat task list hides how different those decisions are.

Context Gets Lost

Without named owners, research links, purchase cautions, room decisions, and small lessons scatter across chats and files. The next workflow then starts from memory instead of evidence.

Automation Can Overreach

A generic assistant may treat a product find, a safety concern, a design idea, and a purchase request as the same kind of task. Las Jaras needs crisp boundaries before anything external happens.

The Model

Jurisdiction

Each being owns a category of care. Sundries watches supplies. Penny Cobalt watches money. Officer Ember watches safety. Mira Queue watches social packaging. This prevents vague ownership.

Voice And Taste

The being's tone encodes the kind of judgment expected. A safety role should be direct. A stylist should care about texture and visual consistency. A treasurer should resist vibes-based spending.

Routing

Ideas move to the right place: product decisions to Penny, installation risk to Ember or Soma, source trails to Archivist Vellum, and public-safe visual inspiration to Mira.

Why It Helps

It Makes The House Legible

Instead of saying "the house needs attention," the model says which part needs attention and why: pantry, guest arrival, garden heat, room styling, safety, memory, or publishing.

It Supports Better Research

Each being can maintain a research beat for its domain. The result is a structured stream of relevant articles, products, references, and cautions instead of random tabs.

It Preserves Human Approval

The beings can prepare decisions without making them. They can sort, summarize, compare, and flag risks while Ilana keeps approval over spending, posting, accounts, public exposure, and final taste.

It Explains The System To Others

The model turns house management into a clear operating chart. A guest, collaborator, contractor, or future agent can understand who handles what and where evidence should go.

Why The Pinterest Workflow Belongs Here

The nightly research-to-Pinterest workflow is a good example of the model. Every being notices what matters inside its lane. Sundries packages useful-object and supply research. Mira Queue handles the public-safe visual layer. Penny Cobalt catches purchase relevance. Officer Ember and Soma catch safety, installation, health, and privacy issues. The same research stream becomes private notes, visual memory, Labs candidates, or rejected noise depending on what it actually is.

Operating Principles

  1. Private note first. Anything that affects a decision needs a durable private source trail.
  2. Visual memory second. Pinterest is useful for inspiration, mood, material, and styling references, but it is not the source of truth.
  3. Human approval stays central. No spending, posting, account creation, new board creation, checkout, publication, or affiliate action without Ilana.
  4. Safety and privacy override charm. Any electrical, health, installation, security, or private-household issue gets routed before it becomes content.
  5. Close the loop. Every useful workflow should leave behind a file, note, packet, checklist, or decision record.
