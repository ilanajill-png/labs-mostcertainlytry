# San Antonio Seed Library 14-Stop Driving Plan

Updated 2026-07-22 for the 14-location seed-library chart, starting and ending at Las Jaras, 406 Brooks Ave, Schertz, TX 78154.

## Route Strategy

This is an ambitious all-day loop. The route starts with the closest Schertz/Garden Ridge stops, sweeps across the north and northwest San Antonio libraries, drops into central/south San Antonio, then returns through the east/northeast stops.

Plan on roughly 4 to 5 hours of driving before visit time, traffic, photos, parking, seed sorting, and breaks. A realistic field day is 7 to 9 hours if you stop at all 14.

## Visit Rules

- Check each public library's hours the morning of the drive.
- Bring seeds only if they are labeled, dry, and appropriate to donate.
- For private-home stops, do not film the house or linger beyond the public seed-library feature.
- After the trip, label every seed packet with location, date, plant name, and any planting notes.
