Know before you go

Austin Tech Week is a paid, badge-based event. Most sessions are first-come, first-served even after registration; adding a session to the official agenda does not guarantee entry.

This is not a free-event list. A Community or Connector+ badge is required for official programming. Satellite events can impose separate registration, eligibility, or cover charges, and the organizer says venues remain subject to capacity.
