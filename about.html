About Ilana Sztaimberg

I make complicated information useful in the places people live.

I am a researcher, storyteller, and project leader exploring how public information, practical technology, and close observation can help people build stronger relationships with nature and place.

The practice

My projects begin with ordinary but consequential questions: What ecological region am I standing in? Where can a neighbor find free seeds? Which plants and practices make sense under South Texas heat? How should a field guide change when the weather itself becomes part of the story?

I work across research, editorial strategy, digital products, community information, and program leadership. Across more than twenty years in communications, brand growth, healthcare, and public-facing experiences, I have learned how to make complex systems understandable—and how to move ambitious work from idea to useful reality.

What I bring

  • Deep source research and careful synthesis
  • Clear public-facing writing and information design
  • Product and program leadership across complex stakeholders
  • Practical tools that give people a meaningful next step
  • Technology used in service of people, place, and sound judgment
