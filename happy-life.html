    How to read the map

    Distance is useful. Lived accessibility requires more.

    Walking highlight

    A destination is highlighted as a potential walk when Valhalla finds a pedestrian route of one mile or less. Confirm sidewalks, crossings, shade, slope, gates, operating hours, wheelchair access, and personal mobility needs before relying on it.

    Driving distance

    Driving mileage and time use the current OpenStreetMap road network. They are estimates, not live traffic predictions. The nearest place by map route may not be the best or most accessible choice.

    Natural water

    A mapped lake, beach, swimming area, or swimming hole is not proof that swimming is permitted or that water quality is safe. Check the current land manager and health advisories.

    Coverage limits

    Bird blinds, butterfly gardens, fishing access, trailheads, galleries, and small local businesses are not mapped uniformly. “Not found” means the current public index returned no suitable candidate—not that none exists.

    Sources and privacy

    Transparent public data, calculated only when requested.

    Facilities

    CMS Care Compare Provider Information, modified September 30, 2026; facility records processed September 1, 2026. The scope is active Medicare/Medicaid-certified nursing homes, not every assisted-living residence.

    Nearby places

    Esri World Geocoding supplies public place candidates. A small curated bird-blind seed list supplements rare-feature coverage. Names, categories, and operating status can change; verify before travel.

    Routing

    Valhalla calculates pedestrian and automobile route estimates over OpenStreetMap. If routing is unavailable, the card shows straight-line distance and says so.

    Privacy

    The tool does not request or store the visitor’s location. Selecting a public facility sends its coordinate and nearby candidate coordinates to map/search/routing services, which may retain standard request logs.
