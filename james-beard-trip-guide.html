James Beard Trip Guide

A reservation-ready shortlist for San Antonio, Houston, Austin, Atlanta, and Massachusetts based on the 2026 James Beard Restaurant & Chef nominees and semifinalists.

Texas: July 3-31, party of 2 Atlanta: June 26-July 2, party of 2 Massachusetts: list + reservation setup links Wine lens: Andre Mack-style, acid-driven, low-oak, food-first
Booking note: I did not actually reserve tables because the request does not include exact dining dates, preferred times, lodging neighborhoods, budget, dietary restrictions, or permission to place bookings in your name. The guide below gives direct reservation links and the dates to target. Once you pick dates/times, book the priority tables first: Mixtli, Nicosi, March, JŪN, Tsuke Edomae, Mujō, and O Ya.

Best First Moves

San Antonio

For July 3-31, book one tasting-menu anchor and one lower-pressure meal.

First choice: Mixtli for the full progressive Mexican tasting menu. Second: Nicosi for Tavel Bristol-Joseph’s dessert-focused room. Backup: Leche de Tigre or The Magpie.

Houston

Houston had the deepest 2026 finalist showing in this set.

First choice: March if the budget allows and reservations are open. Second: JŪN or ChòpnBlọk for food with a strong point of view. Backup: Agnes and Sherman, Maximo, Katami, Xochi.

Austin

No Austin restaurants advanced to the 2026 finalist list, so these are semifinalist picks.

First choice: Tsuke Edomae if you can get seats. Second: Barley Swine or Este. Casual stars: P Thai’s, la Barbecue, Mercado Sin Nombre, La Santa Barbacha, Lao’d Bar.

Atlanta

For June 26-July 2, book fine dining early.

First choice: Mujō for J. Trent Harris’s Best Chef: Southeast finalist run. Second: Aria for hospitality and a classic Atlanta room. Drinks: Kimball House, Avize, Lucky Star, Madeira Park.

Massachusetts

No Massachusetts restaurants advanced to the 2026 finalist list, but the semifinalist pool is strong.

First choice: O Ya for a celebratory splurge. Second: Comfort Kitchen, Tonino, Lenox Sophia, Lê Madeline, La Padrona, Verveine, Talulla, State Road.

Restaurant Notes

Each card includes the Beard recognition, why it is worth going, what to order or expect, a wine order shaped by Andre Mack’s usual food-first logic, and four research links. “Press links” are built to surface current articles/reviews from reliable food publications and local press.

Core sources: James Beard Foundation 2026 nominees/finalists and semifinalists; local finalist coverage from San Antonio Express-News, CultureMap Houston, KUT Austin, Eater Atlanta, and Boston Magazine. Wine guidance draws from Food & Wine’s profiles of Andre Mack, especially his acid-forward, low-oak, food-as-center pairing approach and his preference for medium-bodied acidic wines with strong flavors.
